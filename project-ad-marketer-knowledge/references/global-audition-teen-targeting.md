# Global audition and teen targeting checks

Use this reference when planning paid social/video ads for K-pop/global audition recruitment, especially when the applicant pool includes minors.

## Core rule

Do not describe ages 13-17 as if they can be targeted the same way as adults.

Split audiences into:

- under 13: do not plan direct ad targeting; use guardian-facing communication if the application rules allow younger participants.
- 13-15: highest policy sensitivity; use broad age/location plus creative relevance, and check country-specific platform restrictions.
- 16-17: still under-18; keep teen ad restrictions in force.
- 18+: detailed targeting, lookalike/lookalike-like segments, and lead objectives are more realistic.
- guardians: use Facebook/YouTube/Meta as explanatory and trust-building support, not only direct applicant recruitment.

## Platform guidance

### Meta: Instagram and Facebook

- Meta teen ad delivery should be framed as age/location-led broad delivery.
- Do not propose interests, activities, lookalikes, retargeting, or detailed behavior targeting as the core way to reach 13-17 users.
- Use detailed interests such as K-pop, dance, vocal, audition, cover dance, entertainment, or agency/artist names only for 18+ if they are available in Ads Manager.
- Lead forms can reduce friction, but for minor audition applications they should usually be limited to pre-registration or guardian inquiry. Final application should go to an official form that can handle age, country, video link, terms, privacy, and guardian consent.
- Facebook is usually a support channel for guardians, detailed explanations, regional reach, and retargeting rather than the primary youth recruitment channel.

### TikTok

- For under-18 audiences in impacted locations, detailed targeting can be restricted. Keep teen campaigns broad and creative-led.
- In Australia, TikTok official guidance says ads will not be shown to audiences under 16.
- Lead Generation objectives may be unavailable when reaching people under 18 in impacted locations. Use Reach, Traffic, or video objectives for minors; keep lead generation to 18+ or guardians.
- Hashtags such as #kpop, #kpopdance, #audition, and #dancechallenge should be described as creative/content direction unless the specific targeting option is confirmed in the account.
- Creator Marketplace and Spark Ads are realistic but require creator approval, usage rights, language/local fit, and enough production lead time.

### Google and YouTube

- Google disables ad personalization for users under 18 and restricts sensitive categories. Do not present custom segments or user-data segments as precise under-18 targeting.
- YouTube content targeting can be used for channels, videos, topics, and keywords, but some conversion-focused video campaign types cannot add content targeting.
- Do not say advertisers can directly target subscribers of HYBE, SM, JYP, YG, or other channels. Use "placements/content candidates" or "custom segment seeds" depending on the feature.
- Custom segments based on keywords, URLs, and apps can be used for adult/eligible audiences, but URL seeds do not mean direct visitors to those sites are targeted.
- Avoid saying "30 seconds to 1 minute non-skippable" as a general YouTube plan. Use skippable in-stream/Shorts/in-feed for longer storytelling; use bumper/non-skippable for short awareness messages.

## Proposal language

Prefer:

- "13-17 audiences will be reached through broad age/location delivery and creative relevance."
- "Detailed interest and behavior targeting will be applied to 18+ applicants and guardian segments where available."
- "Country-specific teen ad restrictions will be checked in each platform account before launch."
- "Final application is recommended through an official form that can collect required consent and application materials."

Avoid:

- "13-17 K-pop interest targeting"
- "Target fans of specific agency channel subscribers"
- "Lead Generation for all teen applicants"
- "Recent 7-day K-pop video completion targeting" unless verified in the live account
- "30 sec to 1 min non-skippable YouTube ads" as a default format

## Required pre-launch checks

- Confirm exact applicant birth-year eligibility and whether under-13 applicants are included.
- Confirm required countries and do not mix non-required expansion countries into the core plan.
- Confirm language versions and local subtitles for each country group.
- Confirm whether an official application form can handle privacy, guardian consent, video upload/link, and country-specific requirements.
- Confirm which targeting options are actually available in the live ad accounts by country.
- Confirm whether the campaign should optimize for reach, traffic, lead, or completed application, and split minors/adults accordingly.

## 2026-07-30 update: Korean youth SNS regulation report

### 1) 새로 학습한 사실

- CJ Mezzomedia's August 2026 media report describes a Korean government proposal under review that could restrict social-media signup for users under 14, require stronger age assurance and parental controls, and limit addictive design/recommendation features for older minors.
- The report also describes a possible administrative fine and a domestic-representative mechanism for overseas platforms.
- This is a media-report summary of a proposed legislative direction, not a confirmed current advertising rule.
- An official Korean government, regulator, or enacted-bill source confirming the full package and final effective date was not located on 2026-07-30.

### 2) 기존 지식에서 수정할 점

- Do not say `한국에서는 14세 미만 SNS 가입이 금지됐다`. The safe wording is `14세 미만 가입 제한과 플랫폼 보호의무 강화 방안이 추진·검토되고 있다`.
- Do not apply the report's proposed fine, age threshold, or platform obligations as a current campaign-operating rule until a bill, enforcement decree, or official platform notice is confirmed.
- Existing platform teen-ad restrictions remain the immediate operating standard regardless of whether the Korean proposal is enacted.

### 3) 실무 적용 원칙

1. Keep under-18 campaigns broad and creative-led where platform rules restrict personalization.
2. Separate minors, 18+ users, and guardians in planning, landing, consent, and KPI logic.
3. For under-14 services, prepare guardian-facing communication and avoid relying on a direct platform-account acquisition funnel.
4. Recheck age eligibility, parental consent, lead-form fields, remarketing eligibility, and platform policy immediately before launch.
5. Treat youth-social-media regulation as an environmental risk and contingency-planning item, not as a current legal fact.

### 4) 제안서/리포트 문장 예시

> 국내에서도 14세 미만의 SNS 가입 제한과 청소년 보호 기능 강화를 위한 제도 논의가 진행되고 있어, 청소년 대상 캠페인은 플랫폼별 미성년자 타게팅 제한뿐 아니라 향후 가입·동의 절차 변화까지 고려해야 합니다. 현재 확정 법령으로 단정하지 않고, 집행 시점의 공식 정책을 재확인하겠습니다.

### 5) 다음 확인 필요사항

- Bill number, proposing ministry/legislator, exact statutory basis, and legislative status.
- Final age thresholds, parental-consent mechanics, fine amount, and enforcement date.
- Scope of covered services and whether messaging, video, community, and game platforms are treated differently.
- Platform-specific Korean notices after any law or enforcement decree is finalized.

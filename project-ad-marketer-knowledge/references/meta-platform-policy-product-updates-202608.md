# Meta platform, policy, and product updates - 2026-08

Use this reference for Meta/Facebook/Instagram proposals or account reviews that mention Meta AI Business Assistant, catalog-centered sales setup, health and wellness policy, Instagram for TV, advertiser verification, AI-ad transparency, or data-use control changes.

## 2026-07-30 update

### 1) 새로 학습한 사실

#### 공식 확인

- Meta AI Business Assistant is expanding as a beta to advertisers and agencies across major global markets and local languages. Meta states that it can be accessed within Ads Manager, Meta Business Suite, and Business Support Home and can provide account-specific performance insights, optimization recommendations, and account support.
  - Official source checked 2026-07-30: https://about.fb.com/br/news/2026/04/meta-ai-business-assistant-expande-globalmente-com-suporte-a-novos-idiomas/
  - The official announcement names APAC and local-language expansion, but it does not list Korea account eligibility individually. Korean availability must still be checked in the actual account.
- Instagram for TV expansion and new viewing formats are an Instagram content-consumption experiment, not a confirmed Korean ad inventory product.
  - The uploaded media report describes availability on Samsung Smart TVs in the United States and tests of episodic, live, and horizontal long-form viewing.
  - Official source checked 2026-07-30: https://about.fb.com/news/2026/06/instagram-for-tv-gets-bigger-with-new-ways-to-watch-together/
- Meta is expanding advertiser verification and stated a goal for verified advertisers to account for 90% of ad revenue by the end of 2026. Verification may be required based on region, policy history, or higher-risk categories.
  - Official source checked 2026-07-30: https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/meta-launches-new-anti-scam-tools-deploys-ai-technology-to-fight-scammers-and-protect-people/
- Meta is expanding ad-transparency information through `About this ad` and AI-related labels, including ads significantly edited with Meta generative AI and, in some cases, third-party AI tools.
  - Official source checked 2026-07-30: https://about.fb.com/news/2025/02/gen-ai-transparency-metas-ads-products/
- Meta announced changes to how activity shared by other businesses is used for content and ad personalization, together with a consolidation of user controls. The first rollout covers the United States and some other countries, with more countries to follow; Korea timing is not confirmed.
  - Official source checked 2026-07-30: https://about.fb.com/news/2026/06/better-personalization-and-changes-to-controls-for-your-activity-from-other-businesses/

#### 제공 파일 기반 사실 - 계정 및 공식 정책 페이지 재확인 필요

### 2) 기존 지식에서 수정할 점

- Do not say MAIBA is available to every Korean account. Use `한국을 포함한 주요 시장에 베타가 순차 확대되고 있으나 계정별 노출 확인이 필요` until the advertiser account shows the icon.
- Do not equate Instagram for TV with a new Korean Meta ad placement. It is currently a viewing-product experiment with region/device limitations.
- Do not say every Sales campaign universally requires an advertiser to actively use catalog ads. The uploaded file describes catalog connection as campaign-level infrastructure, but actual account UI, catalog eligibility, and the option to use catalog data for optimization must be checked.
- Do not interpret a claims-based Health and Wellness policy as unrestricted approval of before/after creative or efficacy claims. Personal-attribute rules, misleading-claim rules, country law, landing-page claims, and age restrictions can still apply.
- Do not present Meta AI recommendations as guaranteed performance improvement. The assistant can shorten diagnosis and support work, but an operator must validate business context, attribution, margins, policy risk, and whether a recommendation is executable.

### 3) 실무 적용 원칙

1. Before a Meta proposal or account transfer, check whether MAIBA, Collage, Consumer lifecycle strategy, and campaign-level catalog selection are visible in the actual Korean ad account.
2. For ecommerce Sales campaigns, audit catalog quality even when catalog cards are not the main creative:
   - product ID/content ID match,
   - price and stock freshness,
   - image quality,
   - product-title quality,
   - Pixel/CAPI purchase-event quality,
   - product-set logic.
3. For health, beauty, supplements, weight-management, and wellness advertisers, review the exact claim across ad copy, image text, video dialogue, and landing page. Approval of a product category does not prove that its claims are compliant.
4. Prepare advertiser identity, business information, domain, payment, and rights evidence in advance for categories likely to receive verification.
5. Treat AI-generated/adapted creative as a transparency and brand-governance issue. Preserve original assets, rights evidence, approved claims, and human review records.
6. Continue using Pixel plus CAPI and first-party/CRM validation where possible, while respecting consent and Meta data-sharing rules. Data-control changes do not authorize new collection or bypass privacy requirements.

## 2026-07-30 update: Meta ad postpaid billing

### 1) 새로 학습한 사실

- `후불 결제` can refer to two different Meta billing structures.
  1. Automatic payments: ad cost accrues first and the registered card/payment method is charged when the payment threshold is reached or on the account's monthly bill date, whichever applies first. Threshold charges can occur multiple times in one month, so this is not the same as one monthly invoice.
  2. Monthly invoicing: eligible/approved businesses receive an invoice for accrued spend and pay under the approved credit terms. It is not a payment option that every advertiser can freely turn on.
- In South Korea, the payment methods displayed depend on the account's country, currency, and the payment choice made when the account was created. Meta's official payment-options page lists cards and Korean local manual methods such as KakaoPay, Toss, and bank-account payment for eligible KRW accounts.
- Official sources checked 2026-07-30:
  - https://www.facebook.com/help/messenger-app/212763688755026/
  - https://www.facebook.com/help/messenger-app/131439120265224/
  - https://www.facebook.com/help/messenger-app/291404291014138/

### 2) 기존 지식에서 수정할 점

- Do not call ordinary card automatic payment `월 후불 정산`. A threshold charge may occur before month-end and more than once.
- Do not promise direct Meta monthly invoicing before eligibility, credit line, currency, legal entity, payment terms, and account access are confirmed.
- Distinguish Meta's billing relationship from an agency's commercial settlement. An agency may pay Meta first and invoice the advertiser later even when the underlying Meta account uses card automatic payments.

### 3) 실무 적용 원칙

1. Confirm which party owns the ad account, payment method, credit line, and tax/billing documents.
2. If the agency prepays media cost, define the credit limit, payment due date, late-payment response, campaign suspension rule, refund/credit handling, and account-transfer settlement in the contract.
3. Set an account spending limit or internal monthly cap because a campaign daily budget alone does not replace billing control.
4. Reconcile Meta payment activity, actual media spend, credits/refunds, agency fee, and tax treatment separately.
5. Keep the advertiser's business and ad-account ownership clear; grant the agency partner access rather than relying on shared personal logins.
6. Do not assume that one corporate card being used across several ad accounts is automatically prohibited. Meta publicly identifies unusual payment activity, repeated chargebacks, payment-term violations, and identity verification as possible restriction reasons, while the card issuer can also decline repeated overseas/online/recurring transactions. Diagnose the restriction level before changing the settlement model.
7. Separate the four common agency settlement structures:
   - advertiser-owned payment method and direct Meta billing; agency invoices only the service fee,
   - advertiser prepays a media deposit to the agency,
   - approved Meta monthly invoicing/credit line used by the agency,
   - an authorized media-buying/reseller arrangement with its own credit and settlement terms.
8. Avoid repeatedly registering the same blocked card or rapidly rotating cards across new accounts. First clear outstanding balances, identify whether the restriction is card-, ad-account-, payment-profile-, or business-level, complete verification, contact the card issuer, and request Meta review where available.

### 4) 제안서/리포트 문장 예시

> 메타 광고비 후불 운영은 대행사가 광고비를 우선 결제한 뒤 월 집행액을 기준으로 광고주에게 정산하는 방식입니다. 메타 자체의 카드 자동결제는 결제 기준금액 도달 시 월중에도 여러 차례 청구될 수 있으므로, 월말 1회 후불 인보이스와는 구분됩니다. 실제 정산 방식과 납부기한은 계약 전 별도로 확정합니다.

# Automotive Wiper Performance Ads Reference

Use this reference for online performance ad proposals for automotive wiper brands or sellers, including CAP-style cases.

## Category Logic

Wipers are not a pure impulse purchase category. Buyers usually need one of four assurances before purchase:

1. The product fits their exact vehicle model, year, and size.
2. The wiping problem will be solved: noise, shaking, streaks, water smear, poor visibility.
3. The product quality is credible enough: silicone, hybrid structure, coating, manufacturer credibility, brand trust.
4. The purchase is not risky: correct options, easy fitment guide, clear replacement instructions, reviews, return handling.

For this reason, performance strategy should not start only from media selection. Start from product registration, product naming, fitment guide, review concentration, and product-page persuasion.

## Naver Shopping Search Strategy

For wiper brands, Naver Shopping Search Ads should usually be the first priority when the objective is ROAS or purchase.

Key principle:

- Product ID structure comes before ad group structure.
- Registered product name, product category, attributes, price, reviews, options, and product detail page quality affect performance.
- Excessive car-model-specific product registration can create duplicate-product risk, review fragmentation, and operational complexity.
- A single broad product can collect reviews, but it may be weak for car-model searches.

Recommended structure:

1. Representative product
   - Covers broad and functional searches such as automotive wiper, vehicle wiper, silicone wiper, hybrid wiper, water-repellent wiper.
   - Options should focus on size, front set, rear add-on, adapter, and compatible configuration.
   - Detail page must include vehicle/model/year fitment information.

2. Key domestic model products
   - Separate only high-volume or high-conversion vehicle models after checking search and sales data.
   - Examples: Grandeur, Sorento, Carnival, Avante, Santa Fe, Sportage, K5.
   - For duplicate risk control, make the product genuinely model-specific: product name, summary, option composition, fitment table, and detail page should all match the target model/generation.

3. Import-car adapter products
   - Import vehicles should not be handled only by manufacturer names such as BMW, Benz, or Audi.
   - Start from model-level compatibility where adapter anxiety is high, such as BMW 5 Series, Benz E-Class, Audi A6, if product compatibility is confirmed.
   - Emphasize adapter inclusion, model/year compatibility, and installation guidance.

4. Rear/refill/commercial products
   - Do not make rear wipers a standalone first-priority group by default.
   - Rear wipers are often better handled as an add-on, set option, or bundle with front wipers unless search demand, margin, or fitment complexity justifies separate promotion.

## Campaign Structure

Initial Naver Shopping Search ad groups can be organized by product role:

- Representative / function type
- Key domestic model kits
- Import adapter / model kits
- Rear, refill, or commercial vehicle secondary products
- Seasonal bid test

Do not assume that placing the same product ID into many ad groups will cover many model keywords. If product-level duplication or exposure limits apply, only one ad may meaningfully show. Treat this as a current-rule item to verify in Naver Ads and SmartStore help before final proposal delivery.

For expert-level proposals, do not stop at "run Shopping Search Ads." Show the actual operation frame:

| Campaign | Ad group | Linked product | Operating intent | Initial budget weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shopping search purchase intent | Representative / function | Representative product | Broad and feature-led searches such as automotive wiper, silicone wiper, hybrid wiper, water-repellent wiper | 30-35% |
| Shopping search purchase intent | Key domestic models | Domestic model-specific products | Sorento, Carnival, Grandeur, Avante, Santa Fe, Sportage, K5 model searches | 30-35% |
| Shopping search purchase intent | Import adapters | Import model/adapter products | BMW 5 Series, Benz E-Class, Audi A6 style searches where adapter anxiety is high | 10-15% |
| Shopping secondary purchase | Rear/refill/set | Rear/refill or add-on products | Add-on purchase and AOV lift rather than first-priority acquisition | 5% |
| Shopping seasonal | Rain/snow bid boost | Best-performing products | Bid and budget boost before rainy season, snow, holiday driving, or car inspection periods | 10-15% |

## Naver Shopping Search Bid and Weighting Logic

Initial settings should usually avoid premature exclusions. Use early data to adjust:

- Device: start with mobile priority but keep PC open for comparison-shopping demand. Raise mobile weighting if mobile CVR and ROAS are stronger; lower PC if it produces clicks without purchases.
- Gender: do not assume only men purchase wipers. Observe both genders at the start. If male 30-50s ROAS is clearly stronger, raise weighting by about 10-20%, but do not suppress female traffic before product-level evidence.
- Age: observe 30-50s first as likely vehicle-owner demand, but evaluate 20s and 60+ with actual purchase and cart data.
- Bidding: maintain exposure for proven products; cap bids for test products. Use DIAD Pro or equivalent rank/bid controls to manage target rank and max CPC by product and query intent.
- Product expansion: promote only search terms or car models with evidence into separate product IDs. Do not expand all car models at once.

Proposal-level example:

> If a Sorento MQ4 product generates carts or purchases from Sorento wiper queries, treat it as a model-intent product and maintain exposure. If the representative product converts from silicone wiper or hybrid wiper queries, operate it as a feature-intent product. If a car-model query creates impressions and clicks but no cart or purchase, do not immediately create a dedicated product for that model.

## Budget Logic

If the total monthly budget starts around KRW 5,000,000:

- Naver Shopping Search Ads: 75-80%
- Meta: 20-25%

Within Shopping Search:

- Representative / function product: about 35%
- Key domestic model products: about 35%
- Import adapter products: about 15%
- Rear/refill/secondary products: about 5%
- Seasonal test reserve: about 10%

For KRW 8,000,000-10,000,000:

- Expand key car-model products.
- Add more structured creative and retargeting tests.
- Begin budget reallocation by ROAS, CVR, AOV, margin, and stock.

For KRW 15,000,000+:

- Add seasonal GFA or Meta scaling.
- Consider Naver Brand Search only if brand search volume or official-store recognition needs justify the fixed cost.

## Meta Strategy

Meta is not the first core ROAS channel for wipers unless the brand already has strong product-market fit, product feed, pixel events, and enough remarketing volume.

For Korean commerce advertisers using Naver SmartStore as the only landing destination, do not recommend Meta as a full conversion-optimization channel without qualification. Meta Pixel and event collection are generally not available in the same way as on an owned mall, so purchase, add-to-cart, product-view, ROAS, and catalog retargeting measurement can be restricted. In that case, Meta can still drive traffic or awareness, but it should not be sold as a clean purchase-performance channel.

Creative is a major performance lever for Meta and display ads in this category. Wiper shoppers are not always actively searching when they see display ads, so the ad must create the replacement need. Current wiper ads often use video or short-form formats, not only static images. Prefer showing rainy visibility, wiping before/after, noise/shaking, and fitment-guide moments rather than only product pack shots.

Decision frame:

- If an owned mall or pixel-installable landing exists: Meta can be tested for purchase conversion, catalog, and retargeting.
- If only SmartStore exists: use Meta conservatively for traffic/awareness, or replace much of the Meta role with Naver DA.
- If both SmartStore and owned mall can be used: recommend a dual-landing structure. Naver Shopping Search goes to SmartStore, while Meta goes to the owned mall or a pixel-installable promotion landing.

Client-facing wording:

> Meta advertising requires a measurable conversion path to be evaluated as a performance channel. If all traffic is sent directly to Naver SmartStore, purchase optimization and ROAS measurement inside Meta can be limited. Therefore, CAP should decide whether to prepare an owned mall landing for Meta conversion campaigns or operate Meta only as a traffic/awareness support channel. If owned-mall preparation is difficult, Naver DA can replace part of Meta's demand-generation role while keeping the campaign closer to the Naver commerce environment.

Initial structure:

- 1 campaign
- 1 ad set
- 3 creatives

Initial creative themes:

1. Rainy visibility / safety
2. Noise, shaking, streaks, and poor wiping problem-solution
3. Vehicle-fit confidence and easy size matching

When preparing proposal documents, reserve visual placeholder space for:

- Meta static image examples
- Meta video/short-form frame examples
- Rainy visibility before/after examples
- Vehicle fitment guide creative examples

Do not start with too many ad sets and creatives on a limited budget. Eight or more creatives can be excessive at the start because data will fragment.

Optimization cadence:

- Week 1: CTR, CPC, landing page views, product views.
- Week 2: add-to-cart, purchase, CPA, ROAS.
- Replace low CTR and low conversion creatives.
- If CTR is high but purchase is low, inspect landing page, fitment guide, product price, and option clarity before blaming the media.

Retargeting should be added only after sufficient site/store traffic and pixel/catalog events are available.

For small-to-mid starting budgets, explain the campaign structure explicitly:

| Stage | Structure | Targeting | Budget split | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1-2 | 1 campaign, 1 ad set, 3 creatives | Automated or broad targeting first, limited by sales region if needed | 100% of Meta budget | Avoid data fragmentation and test core messages |
| Weeks 3-4 | Keep winners and replace one creative | Keep broad/automated targeting; observe age/gender reports | 70% winners / 30% test | Identify the effective message among rain, noise, and fitment |
| After data volume | Add retargeting ad set | Product viewers, cart abandoners, site visitors if pixel/catalog data is sufficient | 70-80% prospecting / 20-30% retargeting | Improve purchase recovery and ROAS |

Avoid starting with 2 ad sets and 6-8 creatives when the Meta budget is about KRW 1,000,000-1,250,000 per month. That usually fragments learning too much.

Meta result interpretation:

- High CTR / low purchase: creative made interest, but landing, fitment guide, price, options, reviews, or shipping may be weak.
- Low CTR / purchase exists: niche but purchase-capable creative; improve hook before stopping.
- Low CTR / low purchase: replace creative.
- High cart / low purchase: inspect price, shipping fee, option complexity, promotions, and reviews.

## Naver DA Expansion

Do not lump all Naver DA into one "GFA" line when presenting a detailed plan. Separate by role:

- Feed-style Naver DA: useful for seasonal demand creation, such as rainy season, snow, holiday driving, and problem-solution creatives. Reuse Meta-proven messages but adapt crops and copy to Naver inventory.
- Dynamic retargeting / NDA-style product retargeting: meaningful only when product feed, tags, events, and enough visitors/product viewers are ready. Use for viewed-product or related-product re-exposure.
- ADVoost-style expansion: use only after Shopping Search identifies proven products and messages. Keep test budget, period, and stop criteria clear.
- Brand Search: use when brand search volume, official-store trust, or Meta/DA-driven brand search lift justifies fixed cost.

For proposal documents, reserve placeholder space for exposure examples:

- Naver Brand Search example
- GFA/ADVoost example
- Naver feed-style ad example
- NDA or dynamic product retargeting example

These examples help non-marketing stakeholders understand where the ad appears and why creative quality matters.

## Client-Facing Positioning

Good proposal wording:

> Wiper purchase is strongly affected by vehicle fitment, size matching, and product trust. Therefore, CAP should not only run Shopping Search Ads, but also align product registration, product names, options, fitment guides, and ad operation. Initial advertising should concentrate on Naver Shopping Search Ads to capture high-intent purchase demand, while Meta should support replacement-need awareness and seasonal demand creation.

Avoid:

- Promising immediate ROAS improvement without product-page and fitment readiness.
- Splitting every car model into separate products from the beginning.
- Treating Meta as the main direct-conversion channel before tracking and audience volume are ready.
- Using a generic premium message that is not backed by actual product material, structure, coating, or manufacturer proof.

## Confirmation Checklist

Before final proposal:

- Actual CAP product line: premium, value, refill, import, rear.
- SmartStore or sales-channel registration structure.
- Product name and option structure.
- Vehicle fitment table availability.
- Main product margin and stock.
- Review volume and price competitiveness.
- Pixel/catalog tracking readiness.
- Seasonal promotion schedule.

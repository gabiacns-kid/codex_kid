Use this reference when explaining Criteo, commerce media, performance DSP, dynamic retargeting, retail media, commerce audiences, or Criteo-style network/display advertising in Korean ad proposals and client-facing media plans.

## Core Positioning

Criteo should not be described only as an old-style banner ad network.

Better framing:

```text
Criteo is a commerce-data-based performance media and retail media platform. For advertisers and agencies, its most practical role is commerce audience targeting, product-feed-based dynamic retargeting, customer acquisition/retention, and retail media execution.
```

Simple Korean proposal wording:

```text
크리테오는 단순 배너 네트워크라기보다, 상품 피드와 커머스 데이터를 기반으로 구매 가능성이 높은 고객에게 디스플레이, 영상, 네이티브, 리테일 미디어 광고를 노출하는 커머스 미디어 플랫폼입니다.
```

## Services Agencies Should Know

### 1. Commerce Growth

Advertiser-side performance DSP solution. Treat this as the broad Criteo performance-media container for advertisers, not as one narrow retargeting product.

Use for:

- new customer acquisition;
- customer retention;
- dynamic retargeting;
- commerce audience targeting;
- product-feed-based display/video campaigns;
- full-funnel commerce performance.

Practical meaning:

- This is the main Criteo product family an online ad agency should understand first.
- It is useful for ecommerce advertisers with enough website traffic, product catalog, and conversion data.
- It can support acquisition, retention, and retargeting, but should not be sold as a guaranteed conversion engine.
- If a client asks "What Criteo can do overall for our mall?", explain Commerce Growth.
- If a client asks only "Can we follow up product viewers or cart abandoners?", explain Dynamic Retargeting instead.

Setup requirements:

- product catalog/product feed;
- Criteo OneTag installation;
- conversion tracking;
- usable website or app purchase path;
- budget large enough to learn beyond a tiny remarketing pool.

### 2. Dynamic Retargeting

Criteo's classic and still important lower-funnel use case. It is narrower than Commerce Growth.

Use for:

- visitors who viewed products but did not purchase;
- cart abandoners;
- browsing users who need personalized product reminders;
- ecommerce advertisers with many SKUs;
- advertisers that want dynamic creatives showing price, stock, and relevant products.

Practical meaning:

- This is often the easiest Criteo concept to explain to clients.
- It works best when product feed quality, tag setup, and site traffic volume are sufficient.
- It is not suitable when the advertiser has no ecommerce site, no product feed, little traffic, or no conversion tracking.
- CRM is not always required for basic dynamic retargeting. Product-view, cart, and purchase events can be collected through Criteo OneTag after installation.
- However, if the advertiser wants to separate past buyers, dormant buyers, VIP buyers, or long-term lifecycle segments, first-party customer data or enough accumulated purchase-event history is required.

Client-facing wording:

```text
크리테오 리타겟팅은 사이트 방문자가 본 상품, 관심 상품, 구매 가능성이 높은 상품을 바탕으로 개인화된 광고를 다시 노출해 구매 재방문을 유도하는 방식입니다.
```

### 3. Commerce Audiences / Customer Acquisition

Use for:

- prospecting beyond site visitors;
- reaching shoppers with high purchase intent;
- category, brand, product preference, demographic, location, and lookalike-like modeling;
- expanding beyond closed platforms such as Meta, Google, Naver, or Kakao.

Practical caution:

- Acquisition can scale reach, but early ROAS may be weaker than retargeting.
- It should be proposed after search/shopping and basic retargeting are already reasonably prepared, or when the advertiser has a clear expansion budget.
- In practical proposal language, treat Commerce Audiences as a targeting and audience solution used inside acquisition, retention, or retargeting campaigns, not as a standalone media placement.

### 4. Customer Retention

Use for:

- repeat-purchase categories;
- CRM or CDP audience upload;
- dormant buyer reactivation;
- LTV improvement;
- cross-sell or upsell messaging.

Setup requirements:

- first-party customer data with consent;
- CRM/CDP connection or customer data upload;
- clear segment definitions such as dormant, repeat, high-value, or category buyers.
- If no CRM/CDP exists, retention can still start only after enough tagged purchase and event data accumulates, but historical dormant-customer targeting will be limited.
- For a serious retention proposal, check whether customer data upload, CDP/CRM integration, consent, and segment definitions are available.

### 5. Commerce Max / Retail Media

Retail media buying platform for advertisers and brands.

Use for:

- brands that sell through major retailers;
- advertisers that want onsite sponsored products, onsite display/video, and offsite display/video;
- retail-media budgets that must be planned and measured across multiple retailers;
- SKU-level retail-media reporting and optimization.

Practical meaning:

- More relevant to brands/manufacturers and larger ecommerce advertisers than very small mall operators.
- Particularly useful when the client sells through retailer platforms and wants to advertise close to the purchase point.
- Difference from Commerce Growth: Commerce Growth mainly drives performance for the advertiser's own site/app across open-web and related media, while Commerce Max is for retail-media campaigns across retailer sites/apps and offsite retail-media inventory.
- "Retailer inventory" means advertising surfaces owned or activated by retailers, such as search result pages, category pages, product detail pages, onsite display/video areas, and offsite display/video using retailer first-party audiences.
- Do not name a specific Korean retailer as Criteo-supported unless current availability is confirmed. Use retailer examples only as conceptual examples.

### 6. Video Advertising and Contextual Advertising

Use as support:

- video for awareness/consideration with performance measurement;
- contextual advertising when interest/commerce context is more useful than direct personal identifiers;
- privacy-first targeting environments where third-party identifiers are constrained.

Do not lead with these for small-budget direct-response clients unless the objective is awareness or upper-funnel support.

Contextual Advertising caution:

- Do not simplify it as only "native ads seen under news articles."
- It is closer to a targeting method and campaign solution that combines page context, URL/category/text/image signals, commerce signals, and product relevance.
- The visible ad may look like display, native, product recommendation, or other network-style creative depending on inventory and format.
- In client explanations, describe it as "context and commerce-signal based display/native-style exposure," not simply as a news native ad.

### 7. Commerce Grid / SSP

Criteo's supply-side product for media owners and publisher monetization.

Agency relevance:

- Usually not the first thing to propose to ordinary advertisers.
- Relevant when the client is a publisher, app owner, commerce platform, retailer, or media owner with sellable ad inventory.
- Also relevant for agencies when activating curated commerce audiences and premium deals across DSPs.
- For ordinary advertisers and most agency proposals, keep Commerce Grid as background knowledge rather than a recommended ad product.

## Key Product Distinctions

| Question | Practical Answer |
|---|---|
| Commerce Growth vs Dynamic Retargeting | Commerce Growth is the broad advertiser-side performance DSP suite. Dynamic Retargeting is one lower-funnel use case/campaign type inside that broader Criteo performance-media context. |
| Commerce Audiences | Mostly an audience/targeting layer used in acquisition, retention, and retargeting. It is not best explained as a separate ad placement. |
| Dynamic Retargeting vs Customer Retention | Dynamic Retargeting mainly follows recent site/product intent such as product views and cart abandonment. Customer Retention targets lifecycle states such as repeat buyer, churn-risk buyer, dormant buyer, or high-value buyer, so first-party customer data is more important. |
| Commerce Growth vs Commerce Max | Commerce Growth is for driving performance for an advertiser's own site/app across open-web style media. Commerce Max is retail media: onsite/offsite campaigns using retailer inventory and retailer first-party data, often for brands selling through retailers. |
| Contextual Advertising | A context plus commerce-signal targeting solution. It can look like native/display network advertising, but the key is not the visual format; it is how the placement and product relevance are selected. |
| Commerce Grid | SSP/supply-side solution. Mostly for publishers, media owners, retailers, and inventory owners, not ordinary advertisers. |

## When Criteo Is Meaningful

Recommend or consider Criteo when:

- the advertiser is ecommerce or product-commerce oriented;
- website/app traffic volume exists;
- product feed/catalog is available;
- purchase or lead conversion tracking is in place;
- dynamic product creatives can improve return visits;
- Naver/Google/Meta/Kakao are already running and additional open-web commerce reach is needed;
- the advertiser wants retargeting, acquisition, retention, or retail media beyond closed platforms;
- budget is enough to avoid thin learning.

## When Criteo Is Weak Or Premature

Be cautious when:

- the advertiser has no website or no conversion path;
- product feed quality is poor;
- traffic volume is too small for retargeting;
- monthly budget is very low;
- the client expects immediate search-like intent performance;
- the business is quote-based B2B with very low site traffic and no catalog;
- conversion tracking and consent setup are not ready;
- creative and product information are not stable.

## How To Explain Criteo Against Naver, Meta, Google

- Naver search/shopping: captures explicit search or shopping intent.
- Meta: creates demand and reaches people by social/creative signals.
- Google: captures search demand and broad intent/context across Google inventory.
- Criteo: uses commerce signals, product feed, and open-web/retail-media inventory to reconnect shoppers and expand commerce performance outside a single closed platform.

Short comparison:

```text
네이버와 구글 검색광고가 '검색한 사람'을 잡는 광고라면, 크리테오는 '상품을 볼 가능성이 있거나 이미 관심을 보인 사람'을 오픈웹과 커머스 지면에서 다시 만나게 하는 광고에 가깝습니다.
```

## Client-Facing Recommendation Wording

```text
크리테오는 단순 노출형 배너 광고라기보다, 상품 피드와 구매 행동 데이터를 활용해 구매 가능성이 높은 고객에게 다시 접근하는 커머스 퍼포먼스 매체로 보는 것이 적합합니다.

따라서 홈페이지 방문자, 상품 조회자, 장바구니 이탈자, 기존 구매자 데이터가 어느 정도 쌓여 있는 쇼핑몰이라면 다이나믹 리타겟팅과 고객 획득 캠페인을 통해 네이버·구글·메타 외 추가 매출 경로를 테스트해볼 수 있습니다.

다만 초기 트래픽이 적거나 상품 피드와 전환 추적이 준비되어 있지 않다면, 크리테오를 먼저 집행하기보다 검색광고와 쇼핑광고로 유입과 구매 데이터를 확보한 뒤 확장 매체로 검토하는 편이 현실적입니다.
```

## Proposal Checklist

Before proposing Criteo, confirm:

- website/app exists;
- ecommerce purchase path or measurable lead path exists;
- product feed/catalog is available;
- Criteo OneTag or equivalent tracking can be installed;
- conversion events can be measured;
- monthly site traffic and product-view volume are enough;
- CRM/CDP/customer upload is allowed if retention is proposed;
- budget is large enough for retargeting plus acquisition if both are proposed;
- creative and product data are stable;
- privacy and consent requirements are covered.

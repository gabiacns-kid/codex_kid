# ADVoost Screen Blog Reference

Use this reference when writing Korean blog posts about Naver ADVoost Screen for GabiaCNS-style content.

## Source Priority

1. Use the user's uploaded ADVoost Screen product deck as the main factual base when the post is based on that deck.
2. Verify drift-prone product details against official Naver Ads/NOSP pages when finalizing public-facing copy.
3. If the deck and official page differ, avoid over-specific wording where possible or use the safer operational wording. Example: if official page says 5 business days but the deck/FAQ says 7 business days, write "stable execution is safest when materials are registered around 7 business days before on-air."

## Positioning

ADVoost Screen should be positioned as a Naver DOOH product that lowers the execution burden of outdoor advertising.

Strong framing:

- "옥외광고를 더 쉽게 집행할 수 있게 해주는 네이버 기반 DOOH 통합 운영 솔루션"
- "대형 LED, 극장, 교통, 생활 밀착형 매체를 하나의 플랫폼에서 운영"
- "매체 리스트업, 소재 변환, 검수, 송출, 리포팅 부담을 줄이는 구조"

Avoid:

- Presenting ADVoost Screen like a direct-response performance ad that guarantees conversions.
- Claiming exact conversion tracking is already fully available when the source says future/TBD or indirect measurement.
- Saying advertisers can freely choose individual media or media weight when the product automatically mixes inventory.

## Core Facts To Keep

- Product name: 애드부스트 스크린(ADVoost Screen)
- Platform: NOSP(Naver Operation Support Platform)
- Product idea: DOOH integrated buying and operation solution.
- Exposure media: large LED, CGV theaters and lobbies, Seoul Express Bus Terminal, taxi-top MOTOV, table-order screens such as T-order, elevators, Metro Gate/subway entrances, and other available DOOH inventory.
- Product types in the deck: Premium, Basic, 서울 3권역.
- Minimum budget in the deck: Basic KRW 1,000,000; Premium KRW 5,000,000; 서울 3권역 KRW 5,000,000.
- Billing: charged per playback; system may display CPM.
- Creative: final exposure is video-based, generally 30 seconds in the deck. Images may be used in registration/AutoClip AI workflows for special resolutions.
- Targeting: city/county/district-level regional targeting. Audience targeting such as office, shopping, traffic, and education is noted as planned/opening.
- Creative review: first Naver review, then final media-owner review.
- Operation limitation: advertisers cannot pick only one specific media or freely adjust each media's exposure share; current optimal mix is automatically composed.
- Reporting: NOSP multidimensional reporting can show exposure by placement/date/time; basic indicators may be available after a delay. Future Naver data combination should be framed as planned/TBD if not confirmed.

## Recommended Blog Structure

1. Title optimized for "애드부스트 스크린".
2. Short greeting in GabiaCNS tone.
3. Plain definition of ADVoost Screen.
4. Why it matters: outdoor ads were hard because media selection, budget, creative variation, review, and reporting were fragmented.
5. What changes: NOSP-based integrated purchase, flexible budget/period, AutoClip AI, region targeting, reporting.
6. Product table: Premium / Basic / 서울 3권역.
7. Media network summary: LED / theater / transport / lifestyle.
8. Execution cautions: video creative, review schedule, no individual media selection, ON/OFF delay, restricted industries.
9. FAQ and CTA.

## Tone

Write for search users who are looking up whether this ad product fits their business.

- Define DOOH in plain Korean.
- Keep sentences easy and practical.
- Use tables to summarize product choices, costs, media, and cautions.
- Emphasize "what to check before execution" instead of exaggerated benefits.
- Use "브랜드 인지도", "지역 커버리지", "생활 동선 노출", "옥외광고 집행 허들 완화" naturally.

## Strategic Positioning

Gmarket/Auction ads should not be presented as one generic open-market ad product. Separate search-intent capture from AI-driven demand expansion:

- **Power Click**: keyword/product based CPC search and shopping-intent advertising. Best for deliberate exposure of priority products on selected keywords, categories, product detail pages, and related shopping contexts.
- **AI SalesUp**: AI-personalized product recommendation advertising. Best for broadening product discovery and retargeting-like exposure across Gmarket/Auction internal surfaces and external partner media with minimal setup.

## 2026 Gmarket Product-Centric Operation Update

Use this section when diagnosing current Gmarket reports or writing weekly insights for Gmarket Power Click after the user's 2026 report discussion.

The current practical view is:

- Treat **Gmarket as product-centric advertising first**, not keyword-control advertising.
- Keywords remain useful as diagnostic context, but they should not be described as the main operating lever when exclusion and granular keyword control are unavailable or limited.
- Gmarket may automatically expand matching keywords based on the registered product.
- If keyword exclusions are unavailable in the actual account/UI, do not propose keyword exclusion as an action.
- If exposure is personalized, search result composition, product count, and product position can differ by user. Do not overstate fixed rank management.
- The realistic control lever is often **product bid strength**, interpreted as adjusting the bid level applied to the product or the highest-cost keyword mapped to that product.
- Weekly action should therefore be framed around product-level bid up/down, product keep/observe/reduce, and product competitiveness improvements.

Critical interpretation:

> Do not say "Gmarket keyword bid up/down" as the main action when the account cannot directly manage keywords that way. Say "adjust product bid strength based on product-level spend, purchases, ROAS, and automatically expanded search context."

Use keywords this way:

- Diagnose which search contexts are spending or converting.
- Understand whether the product is being matched to broad or low-intent demand.
- Inform product title, thumbnail, price/benefit, detail-page, and product-group decisions.
- Identify whether a product's automatic expansion is too broad.
- Avoid presenting keyword reports as if every keyword can be excluded, isolated, or ranked manually.

Product-level operating framework:

| Product status | Signal | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Scale | Purchase and ROAS are strong, but spend/click volume is still limited | Maintain or slightly raise product bid strength |
| Keep | Purchase and ROAS are acceptable, spend is stable | Keep bid and monitor |
| Observe | Clicks/spend exist but data is thin | Keep short test window and review next week |
| Reduce | Meaningful spend with no purchase, or ROAS below target | Lower product bid strength |
| Exclude/stop if possible | Repeated high spend with no purchase across multiple weeks | Pause product, remove from ad set, or sharply reduce bid if product exclusion is available |

For weekly reports, write Gmarket insights in this order:

1. Summarize market-level performance.
2. Separate product groups into high-efficiency, low-efficiency, and observation.
3. Mention keyword data only as "search context" or "automatic matching signal."
4. Recommend product bid strength changes.
5. Add product-page competitiveness checks when poor conversion may be caused by price, reviews, benefit, thumbnail, detail page, or stock.

Client-facing wording:

> Gmarket ads are currently closer to a product-centered exposure structure than a manually controlled keyword campaign. Because matching and exposure can expand automatically from the product and may be personalized by user, weekly operation should focus less on individual keyword exclusion and more on product-level bid strength. We will reduce bid strength for products that accumulated cost without purchases, and maintain or slightly increase bid strength for products with confirmed purchases and strong ROAS. Keyword data will be used as a diagnostic signal to understand the search context, not as the primary control lever.

## 2026 AI Product Ads Transition

Use this section when the user asks about the 2026 Gmarket ad-center change, migration from Power Click / AI SalesUp, or how to explain `AI Product Ads` to sellers.

Current blog-based summary:

- The GabiaCNS post says existing Power Click and AI SalesUp ads are expected to transition into the new AI-based `AI Product Ads` structure with a new ad center in June 2026.
- Treat this as a high-risk, time-sensitive product update. Before client delivery, verify final launch dates, migration menus, benefits, eligibility, and reporting behavior in the official Gmarket ad center or direct partner notice.
- The stable strategic shift is from manual keyword/rank control toward product-data-based AI optimization.
- Billing is still described as CPC: cost occurs on actual clicks, not impressions.
- Exposure is described as expanding across search and recommendation areas, including search results, Gmarket home, and product detail pages, but actual inventory can change.
- Fixed rank bidding is not the promise. The blog explicitly warns that there is no old-style "fixed No. 1 exposure" rank-bidding function.

Core positioning:

> AI Product Ads should be explained as a product-data and AI-optimization ad structure, not as a simple rename of Power Click. The seller still controls the product/budget strategy depending on campaign type, but AI handles more keyword expansion, placement selection, and bid/exposure optimization than the old manually managed structure.

### Three Operating Types

| Type | Best for | Seller controls | AI controls | Operating caution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Integrated operation | Many SKUs, beginner sellers, limited operation time | Mainly ad-group daily budget | Keyword creation/expansion, bidding, exposure optimization | Product data quality becomes the main lever: title, category, representative image, price, reviews, benefits |
| Focused operation | Priority SKUs, new products, seasonal products | Product selection, auto/max bid, daily budget | Keyword recommendation/mapping and bid optimization within settings | Choose products deliberately. Separate proven high-margin/reviewed products from new/event products |
| Direct operation | Experienced sellers needing keyword/product/bid intervention | Product, exposure area, keyword bid, daily budget | Keyword expansion can still occur | Expanded keyword-level performance may not be provided, so KPI should shift toward campaign/product-level ROAS and CPA |

Practical recommendation:

- Do not recommend one operating type mechanically.
- Use integrated operation for broad SKU coverage and low management resources.
- Use focused operation when there are specific hero SKUs or seasonal pushes.
- Use direct operation only when the seller has enough margin, product volume, and operating skill to review outcomes without granular expanded-keyword reporting.

### Learning Window and Budget Changes

- The current official migration guide says the initial learning period varies by seller sales status, campaign characteristics, and scale, and can take at least one week. Do not present 2-4 weeks as a fixed platform rule.
- Gmarket manager guidance shared during beta on 2026-07-15 says changing the daily budget does not itself trigger a formal relearning cycle.
- This does not mean budget changes have no performance impact. Budget size still changes auction participation, spend pace, and the amount and mix of learning data collected.
- When evaluating a budget change, separate the pre-change and post-change periods and avoid interpreting an immediate delivery shift as proof that the algorithm has reset.
- For seasonal or launch campaigns, start early enough to secure at least the minimum learning and observation period before peak demand, but do not promise a universal number of weeks.

Client-facing wording:

> 공식 가이드상 AI Product Ads의 초기 학습 기간은 판매아이디의 판매 현황과 캠페인 규모에 따라 달라지며 최소 1주 이상 소요될 수 있습니다. G마켓 안내상 일예산 변경 자체가 재학습을 새로 시작시키는 것은 아니지만, 예산 규모는 노출·클릭·전환 데이터의 축적 속도와 소진 흐름에 영향을 줍니다. 따라서 예산 변경 전후 성과를 구분해 확인하고, 단기 변동만으로 상품과 캠페인을 성급하게 교체하지 않는 운영이 필요합니다.

## Power Click

Power Click is the more controllable operating product. It requires deliberate group, product, keyword, budget, exposure, and bid management.

### Registration Structure

General registration flow:

1. Group and product setup.
2. Keyword selection.
3. Ad strategy setup.
4. Bid information input.

Important operating details:

- Group names can be newly created or selected from existing groups; duplicate group names cannot be used.
- Only eligible on-sale products can be selected. Sold-out, unavailable, or site-mismatched products can fail registration.
- Keywords can be searched or recommended, and recent one-month keyword data can be referenced.
- Bulk registration uses Excel 97-2003 `.xls`; large-scale registration can handle up to 20,000 ads at once in the uploaded guide.
- Required bulk fields include seller ID, site, ad group name, keyword name, product number, and desired click cost.

Proposal implication:

- Power Click is suitable when the advertiser has priority SKUs, target keywords, and enough margin/stock to support paid traffic.
- It is not a "set once and forget" product; keyword and product management are the value of agency operation.

### Budget and Exposure Control

Budget controls:

- Daily allowed budget can be set by group and by site/seller ID.
- Minimum daily budget in the uploaded guide is KRW 1,000.
- Auto budget management can add predefined budget when daily spend reaches a configured threshold.
- Daily budget changes may apply the next day in some simple-registration cases, while auto budget management can apply immediately.

Exposure controls:

- Exposure period, weekday, and time can be configured.
- Exposure can extend beyond Gmarket/Auction search result pages to category pages and product detail pages.
- Simple registration can use excluded keywords to prevent unwanted recommended keyword exposure.

Operating caution:

- Budget expansion should be tied to product stock, price competitiveness, review quality, coupon/benefit condition, and margin.
- If the advertiser only asks for "more exposure," push back and define which SKUs and which keyword/category contexts deserve spend first.

### Bidding and Automation Limits

Key bid rules from the uploaded guide:

- Desired click cost is entered in KRW and usually in 10 KRW units.
- Minimum desired click cost appears as KRW 90 in the uploaded bulk-management example.
- Gmarket product bidding can use **bid-price basis** only; desired-rank basis is not available for Gmarket products in the uploaded guide.
- Auction products can use desired-rank based bidding and auto-bidding settings.
- Auction products can provide average-rank metrics; Gmarket products do not provide average exposure-rank metrics in the uploaded guide.

Critical proposal wording:

> G마켓 파워클릭은 네이버 검색광고처럼 모든 상품에서 희망순위 기준 자동입찰을 전제로 설명하면 안 됩니다. 업로드 자료 기준으로 G마켓 상품은 입찰가 기준 운영이 중심이며, 희망순위 기준 입찰과 평균순위 지표는 옥션 상품 중심으로 확인됩니다.

## AI SalesUp

AI SalesUp is a personalized recommendation ad product designed for easy setup and broad discovery.

### Product Role

AI SalesUp is positioned for sellers who:

- Hit a growth ceiling by operating only a few priority products through Power Click.
- Want to expose products to interested customers beyond direct search.
- Need low-effort ad operation because seller resources are limited.
- Are uncomfortable with complex keyword/bid operation.

Core role:

- Gmarket/Auction AI analyzes customer behavior and preference signals.
- It recommends products likely to match customer purchase intent.
- It exposes product information in formats optimized to each surface.
- It automatically charges CPC while considering exposure position, ad performance, and customer reaction.

Setup flow:

1. Select seller ID.
2. Set daily allowed budget.
3. Set ad exposure period.

Operational setup notes from the uploaded guide:

- Registration starts by selecting a seller ID with available registration status.
- Daily allowed budget controls the upper spend limit for 24 hours.
- The operating guide shows daily budget can be set as unlimited or as a fixed amount.
- When setting a fixed daily budget, the guide indicates a minimum of KRW 10,000.
- Auto budget management can increase budget automatically when a selected share of daily budget is spent.
- Exposure period can be set as continuous exposure from the next day or as a defined start/end date.

Optimization note:

- The uploaded guide says sufficient budget and at least two weeks of ad execution are needed for AI optimization.
- "No budget limit" and "continuous exposure without end date" are presented as expected to produce stronger efficiency, but this should be recommended only when stock, margin, and spend risk are acceptable.

Agency operating caution:

- Do not recommend unlimited budget as a default. Use it only when the seller has enough inventory, margin, product-page readiness, and spend tolerance.
- If the advertiser is testing AI SalesUp for the first time, define a minimum two-week learning window and a daily spend guardrail before scaling.
- Budget automation should be explained as a spend-continuity tool, not as a performance guarantee.

### Exposure Surfaces

Internal and external surfaces in the uploaded introduction include:

- Home main.
- Best.
- My G.
- Attendance/check-in areas.
- Search results.
- Product detail pages.
- Cart.
- External partner web/app inventory such as portals, communities, news, specialist media, and apps.

Important caution:

- Some PC/Auction surfaces or inventory can be added later or changed.
- Partner media can be expanded or ended depending on traffic efficiency.
- Do not promise fixed surfaces without current official confirmation.

### Eligibility and Exclusions

Ad exposure is limited to products satisfying conditions such as:

- Seller ID/ad group is ON.
- Product status is "on sale."
- Product has not been blacklisted/excluded in the AI SalesUp menu.

Operational note:

- Sold-out products can be removed from ad exposure, but the guide notes a delay of roughly 30 minutes to 2 hours may occur.
- Seller-side stock and product status management matters even for AI-operated ads.

### Management and Status Checks

AI SalesUp management should monitor seller ID, group name, ON/OFF state, operating status, clicks, average CPC, current spend, exposure period, ad product count, and total product count.

Common status or blocking reasons shown in the uploaded guide include:

- Operating.
- Stopped.
- Not in exposure period.
- Insufficient deposit/prepaid balance.
- Already operating or already stopped when trying to start again.

Operating implication:

- If AI SalesUp does not spend, do not assume algorithm failure first. Check seller ID eligibility, ON/OFF status, exposure period, deposit balance, ad-eligible product count, and excluded products.
- Because AI SalesUp depends on eligible product inventory, product status and stock hygiene are part of ad operation.

### AI SalesUp Reporting

Report views:

- Date-level report.
- Seller/group-level report.

Search conditions:

- All seller IDs.
- Specific seller ID.
- Basic period appears as recent seven days.
- Detailed calendar period can be selected.
- Reports are generated based on statistics through the previous day.

Key report metrics:

- Clicks.
- Average CPC including VAT.
- Total cost including VAT.
- Order count.
- Purchase quantity.
- Purchase amount.
- Conversion rate.
- Ad revenue rate/ROAS.

Attribution distinction:

- **Ad product basis**: purchase metrics for the clicked advertised product within 15 days after an ad click.
- **Seller basis**: purchase metrics for the seller's products within 15 days after an ad click.

Client-facing interpretation:

> AI매출업 리포트는 광고 상품 기준과 판매자 기준을 구분해 봐야 합니다. 광고 클릭 후 15일 이내 발생한 구매를 기준으로 보되, 클릭한 상품 자체의 구매 성과와 같은 판매자의 다른 상품 구매 성과가 다르게 잡힐 수 있으므로 ROAS 해석 시 기준을 명확히 해야 합니다.

Proposal caution:

- Do not compare AI SalesUp ROAS with Power Click ROAS without checking whether both are using the same attribution basis.
- Seller-basis performance can look broader than ad-product-basis performance because it includes purchases across the seller's products after the ad click.
- For client reporting, state whether figures are ad-product basis or seller basis.

## Power Click vs AI SalesUp

Use this simple distinction in proposals:

| Need | Recommended Product | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Priority SKU exposure on selected keywords | Power Click | Manual control over products, keywords, bids, groups, and exposure settings |

| Seller lacks operating resources | AI SalesUp | Setup is simpler and AI handles exposure optimization |
| Need product discovery beyond search | AI SalesUp | Personalized recommendation across internal and external surfaces |
| Need both intent capture and reach expansion | Power Click + AI SalesUp | Power Click captures active search demand; AI SalesUp expands personalized discovery |

> AI매출업은 자동화 상품이지만, 자동화가 곧 무조건적인 성과 보장을 의미하지 않습니다. 충분한 예산과 최소 학습 기간, 판매 가능한 상품 상태, 가격·리뷰·혜택 경쟁력이 함께 준비되어야 합니다.

## 2026-06-23 Daily Learning: Product-Centered Sales Email Framing

Source context: `G마켓 전용` thread updated sales email drafts for Livmom, Ruave, and TrueTrade. The reusable learning is not the advertiser names themselves, but the way Gmarket's changed operating structure should be explained in outreach emails.

### Newly Learned Facts

- Gmarket outreach should not be framed as "we will manage keywords better" as the main promise.
- The stronger frame is that Gmarket has moved from keyword-centered operation toward product-centered operation, where product data, AI recommendation exposure, and product-level ROI management matter more.
- A simple three-stage explanation works well in client emails:
  - Previous structure: keyword bidding, keyword rank, keyword-by-keyword performance management.
  - Current structure: product-centered ad operation, product data influence, AI recommendation exposure, product-level performance management.
  - Future structure: search plus recommendation plus AI-based exposure, lower direct keyword control, stronger product competitiveness impact.
- Client emails should connect this structural shift to the advertiser's business type:
  - Seasonal bedding brands: cooling pads, summer bedding, and seasonal SKUs require fast product selection and budget concentration.
  - Brands with clear seasonal demand: rank should not be treated as the only objective; product conversion efficiency and ROAS must be reviewed together.
  - Wholesale or large-SKU sellers: broad exposure can scatter budget across weak or duplicated products, so product selection and group simplification matter first.

### Knowledge to Revise

- Avoid overemphasizing "keyword expansion" for Gmarket when direct keyword control is limited or when product-centered matching dominates.
- Keyword/rank language can still be used as a historical comparison or supporting context, but the agency value should be repositioned as:
  - product selection,
  - product group simplification,
  - bid-strength and budget concentration by SKU,
  - product-level reporting,
  - time-of-day bid adjustment,
  - product-page improvement diagnosis.
- If an email says GabiaCNS can refer to keyword-level average rank trends, word it carefully as an internal/reference signal, not as guaranteed direct keyword rank control.

### Practical Operating Principles

1. Start with the platform change.
   - Explain that Gmarket advertising is no longer only about securing keyword rank.
   - This helps the advertiser understand why a new agency can add value even if the existing campaign is already running.

2. Translate the change into the advertiser's category.
   - For seasonal products, emphasize seasonal SKU concentration and quick decision-making.
   - For high-SKU sellers, emphasize SKU pruning and avoiding budget dispersion.
   - For brand sellers, emphasize product competitiveness, thumbnail, price, reviews, and detail-page readiness.

3. Propose three concrete checks.
   - Ad group and product structure cleanup.
   - Product-level performance reporting.
   - Bid/rank/time-of-day operation based on CPC and conversion efficiency.

4. Use readable email formatting.
   - Use short sections, bullets, and numbered lists.
   - Avoid long paragraphs, especially when explaining the Gmarket structural shift.

## Industry-Specific Field Tactics After Product-Centric Expansion

Treat the following as observed operating patterns, not universal best practices. Apply them only after checking hourly and product-level spend, conversion revenue, ROAS, and margin.

### Wholesale and B2B Sellers

- Some wholesale sellers experienced weaker exposure quality and a sharp ROAS decline after product information began influencing a wider range of matched queries.
- These sellers may already turn ads off on weekends and may extend the defensive schedule by stopping exposure after 17:00 or 18:00 on weekdays.
- Do not present this as the default wholesale strategy. It is an aggressive loss-control measure for accounts where late-hour spend repeatedly produces insufficient conversion value.
- Before a full OFF decision, compare three options:
  1. Lower bids by time of day to preserve limited exposure while controlling CPC.
  2. Restrict operation to products with confirmed margin and conversion performance.
  3. Turn the account or selected product groups off only during repeatedly unprofitable hours.
- Evaluate wholesale accounts with contribution margin or break-even ROAS where possible. A nominal ROAS improvement can still be unprofitable when product margins are low.

Client-facing interpretation:

> 도매 상품은 등록 상품 수가 많고 상품명·상품정보를 기반으로 노출 범위가 넓어질 수 있어, 개편 이후 비용 분산과 비효율 노출 위험이 상대적으로 커질 수 있습니다. 실제로 수익률 방어를 위해 주말뿐 아니라 평일 17시 또는 18시 이후 광고를 중단하는 강한 운영도 사용되고 있습니다. 다만 일괄 OFF를 먼저 적용하기보다 상품별 수익성과 시간대별 전환 성과를 확인한 뒤 입찰가 하향, 운영 상품 축소, 비효율 시간대 OFF 순으로 단계적으로 대응하는 것이 적절합니다.

### Health Supplements

- Automatic keyword expansion can make manual keyword scrubbing, exclusion, and exact query attribution difficult.
- Some accounts isolate competitor-brand terms or competitor-related traffic in a separate group and operate that group at a lower bid than general or own-brand groups.
- The purpose is not to promise competitor-keyword conquest. It is a low-bid exploratory segment intended to test whether expanded exposure can produce incremental conversions at an acceptable CPC and ROAS.
- Because Gmarket can combine or obscure bid-keyword and expanded-keyword performance, it may be impossible to confirm whether a conversion came from the explicitly managed competitor term or another expanded query.
- Judge the tactic at the product or group level using spend, average CPC, conversions, conversion revenue, CVR, and ROAS. Do not claim keyword-level causality without evidence.
- Continue to avoid prohibited disease-treatment or improvement claims in registered keywords, product titles, and detail pages.

Client-facing interpretation:

> 건강기능식품은 자동 확장으로 인해 유입 키워드를 정교하게 정리하거나 성과 발생 검색어를 완전히 분리하기 어렵습니다. 이에 따라 타사 브랜드 관련 노출을 무조건 차단 대상으로 보기보다 별도 그룹에서 낮은 입찰가로 제한 운영하고, 상품 또는 그룹 단위의 평균 CPC·전환율·ROAS를 통해 효율을 확인하는 방식도 활용할 수 있습니다. 다만 전환이 직접 등록한 타사 키워드에서 발생했는지, 자동 확장된 다른 검색어에서 발생했는지는 명확히 구분하기 어려우므로 키워드 단위 성공 사례로 단정해서는 안 됩니다.

## Generalized Strategy Framework for the Current Gmarket Environment

Do not stop at collecting tactics by industry. The reusable lesson from wholesale time-based OFF and health-supplement low-bid competitor groups is that reduced keyword control requires a different operating system.

### Structural Diagnosis

The current environment creates four practical problems:

1. **Exposure uncertainty**
   - Product titles, categories, attributes, brands, and other product information can widen matching.
   - The advertiser may not be able to identify or exclude every expanded query.

2. **Attribution uncertainty**
   - Keyword reports may combine directly managed and expanded traffic.
   - A conversion cannot always be attributed to the exact keyword or matching route that caused it.

3. **Rank and bid uncertainty**
   - Fixed keyword rank is not a reliable operating promise.
   - Bid increases can raise CPC without producing a stable or measurable rank gain.

4. **Budget dispersion**
   - Many products, overlapping products, and broad matching can split spend across low-value inventory.
   - The account can appear active while insufficient data accumulates on each product.

The strategic response is therefore:

> Move the decision unit from individual keywords to product, product cluster, time window, and profitability tier.

### Diagnose by Business Characteristics, Not Only Industry Labels

Before choosing a tactic, score the advertiser on these dimensions:

| Dimension | Low-risk condition | High-risk condition | Strategic implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product count | Few focused SKUs | Hundreds or thousands of SKUs | High-SKU accounts need product selection and group simplification first |
| Margin | High contribution margin | Low margin or price competition | Low-margin accounts need break-even ROAS and stronger spend controls |
| Search-intent purity | Queries usually indicate purchase intent | Informational, ingredient, disease, repair, wholesale, or unrelated intent mixes together | Mixed intent requires lower exploratory bids and product-level validation |
| Product overlap | Products have clearly different roles | Similar titles, thumbnails, and configurations compete with one another | Reduce internal cannibalization and duplicate paid clicks |
| Seasonality | Demand is stable | Short peak season or event dependency | Start learning early, then concentrate budget on proven seasonal products |
| Brand strength | Strong own-brand demand | Distributor, wholesaler, or weak brand recognition | Use own-brand framing only for real brand sellers; wholesalers use registered-product brand and model context |
| Regulation and claims | Few expression restrictions | Health, medical, food, or regulated claims | Treat expansion as a compliance-monitoring risk as well as an efficiency risk |
| Product-page competitiveness | Strong price, package, title, image, and persuasion | Clickable but weak conversion readiness | Do not solve a product-page problem only with lower or higher bids |

This allows the same framework to be applied across industries:

- **Fashion and bedding:** separate seasonal hero products, evergreen products, and test products. Concentrate before the peak instead of advertising the full catalog evenly.
- **Food and fresh products:** split by purchase cycle, perishability, package size, and margin. Control spend on low-margin or shipping-sensitive products.
- **Beauty and cosmetics:** separate own-brand, ingredient or function, competitor-related, and broad discovery contexts. Evaluate claims and detail-page persuasion together.
- **Electronics and appliances:** organize around brand, model, specification, and replacement demand. Prevent similar models from competing for the same traffic.
- **Household goods and furniture:** separate generic need, material or size, room or usage context, and seasonal replacement demand. Use product clusters rather than excessive keyword groups.
- **Wholesale and multi-category sellers:** prioritize profitable product clusters and operating hours; do not treat all listed products as equally advertiseable.
- **Health supplements:** use low-bid exploratory groups for ambiguous or competitor-related traffic, but judge results at product or group level and monitor claim risks.

### Four Strategic Modes

Choose a mode based on evidence. An account can use more than one mode across different product groups.

#### 1. Defend

Use when ROAS has fallen, margin is thin, or exposure has become too broad.

- Reduce advertised products.
- Lower bids by product and time.
- Stop repeatedly unprofitable product groups or hours.
- Protect products with confirmed conversion and margin.
- Use full OFF only when softer controls repeatedly fail.

#### 2. Explore

Use when automatic expansion creates new traffic but the source of value is uncertain.

- Create a small, separately budgeted test group.
- Use lower bids than core product groups.
- Test one hypothesis at a time: competitor context, broad category demand, new product, or new season.
- Judge by product/group CPC, CVR, revenue, ROAS, and margin rather than keyword-level causality.

#### 3. Concentrate

Use when too many products or groups divide the budget.

- Merge groups that do not require different budgets, bids, schedules, or evaluation rules.
- Classify products as core, growth, test, hold, and stop.
- Avoid operating multiple near-identical products aggressively on the same demand unless shelf-share is an intentional and affordable objective.
- Move budget toward products with both conversion evidence and competitive product information.

#### 4. Scale

Use only after a product or cluster proves that additional spend can produce profitable volume.

- Increase bids or budgets gradually.
- Preserve enough time for learning and comparison.
- Expand from core products to adjacent products or recommendation surfaces.
- Monitor marginal ROAS, not only total ROAS. Stop scaling when the additional budget buys increasingly weak traffic.

### Operating Sequence

Use the following sequence for account restructuring:

1. **Set the economic boundary**
   - Define margin, break-even ROAS, daily loss tolerance, inventory, and target sales volume.

2. **Clean the product structure**
   - Identify duplicate, overlapping, low-margin, out-of-stock-risk, weak-review, and weak-page products.

3. **Simplify groups**
   - Keep separate groups only when product role, budget, bid, schedule, margin, or evaluation rule is genuinely different.

4. **Assign strategic modes**
   - Defend, explore, concentrate, or scale by product cluster.

5. **Use time as a control layer**
   - Compare hourly CPC, spend, conversion, revenue, and ROAS.
   - Prefer bid adjustment before blanket OFF, but allow OFF where repeated losses justify it.

6. **Read keywords as context**
   - Use own-brand, registered-product brand/model, general, competitor-related, and expanded terms as diagnostic categories.
   - Do not promise exact exclusion, rank, or attribution when the platform does not provide it.

7. **Make product-level decisions**
   - Maintain, raise, reduce, hold, or stop products based on spend, average CPC, CVR, revenue, ROAS, and margin.

8. **Connect advertising to product readiness**
   - If clicks are sufficient but conversion is weak, review title, thumbnail, price/package, product-page persuasion, and internal product overlap before blaming bid settings.

### How Far the Strategy Should Go

The agency should go beyond reporting and bid changes, but should not pretend to control what the platform does not expose.

Appropriate strategic scope:

- Account and group restructuring.
- Product portfolio selection.
- Product-role classification.
- Product- and time-based bid control.
- Break-even and marginal ROAS management.
- Controlled exploratory groups.
- Product title, thumbnail, price/package, and detail-page diagnosis.
- Clear action proposals from product-centered reporting.

Inappropriate promises:

- Exact control over every expanded keyword.
- Guaranteed fixed keyword rank.
- Precise attribution of conversions to hidden expanded queries.
- Performance recovery through time OFF alone.
- Scaling all products because total account ROAS looks acceptable.

The mature operating objective is:

> Build an account that can absorb imperfect keyword control by limiting downside, isolating uncertainty, concentrating data and budget on meaningful product units, and scaling only where marginal profitability is confirmed.

## Product-Data-First Diagnosis and GabiaCNS Reporting

Do not describe the operating shift as abandoning keyword data. Keyword data remains useful for interpreting traffic context, but product data should be the primary decision basis.

Why product data has priority:

- Automatic keyword expansion can make the full matched-query path difficult to identify.
- Direct and expanded keyword performance may not be cleanly separated.
- Fine keyword-rank control and exact rank verification can be limited.
- The advertised product is still the unit that accumulates cost, clicks, conversions, revenue, and ROAS.
- Product-level decisions remain actionable even when keyword-level attribution is incomplete.

Use the data layers this way:

| Data layer | Role | Do not overstate |
|---|---|---|
| Keyword data | Interpret brand, general, competitor-related, broad, or unexpected traffic context | Exact keyword causality, complete exclusion, or fixed-rank control |
| Product data | Decide maintain, raise, reduce, hold, or stop | A product-level result does not alone explain why the user searched |
| Time data | Control hourly CPC, spend, conversion, and loss exposure | A low-performing hour is not automatically a permanent OFF target |
| Product-readiness data | Diagnose title, thumbnail, price/package, reviews, and detail-page persuasion | Listing improvement is not the same as direct ad-platform control |

GabiaCNS proposal positioning:

> 자동화 광고에서는 확장된 키워드의 전체 경로를 확인하기 어렵고, 키워드별 순위를 과거처럼 세밀하게 조정하거나 고정적으로 확인하는 데에도 한계가 있습니다. 따라서 키워드 데이터는 어떤 검색 맥락에서 상품이 유입되고 있는지 파악하는 보조 지표로 활용하고, 실제 운영 결정은 상품별 광고비, 클릭수, 평균 CPC, 전환수, 전환율, 매출, ROAS를 기준으로 진행해야 합니다. 가비아CNS는 이미 상품 중심으로 성과를 확인할 수 있도록 리포트를 가공해 제공하고 있으며, 이를 토대로 상품별 유지·확대·입찰 하향·관찰·중단과 다음 운영 액션까지 함께 제안합니다.

### How to Analyze Advertiser Conditions

Exclude regulation and expression risk from the default industry diagnosis unless the category specifically requires a policy review.

#### 1. Product Count and Product Overlap

Data and checks:

- Total listed products versus products currently spending ad budget.
- Number of products receiving meaningful clicks or conversions.
- Similarity of product titles, thumbnails, options, price ranges, and intended demand.
- Duplicate exposure of similar products in the same search context.
- Spend and conversion concentration among the top products.

Interpretation:

- Many listed products do not automatically justify advertising many products.
- If many products spend small amounts without enough conversion data, simplify groups and concentrate the budget.
- If similar products compete for the same demand, select a main product and assign the others as test, hold, or complementary products.

#### 2. Margin and Break-Even ROAS

Required advertiser inputs:

- Product selling price.
- Product cost and marketplace fee.
- Shipping and packaging cost.
- Discount or event cost borne by the seller.
- Approximate contribution margin.

Calculations:

- Break-even ROAS = revenue divided by allowable advertising cost.
- Allowable ad cost per order = contribution profit before advertising.
- Compare actual product ROAS with break-even ROAS, not with one universal account target.

Interpretation:

- A product with lower reported ROAS can still be viable if its margin is high.
- A low-margin product can remain unprofitable even when its ROAS appears acceptable.
- If margin data is unavailable, label profitability conclusions as provisional and use sales-efficiency indicators only.

#### 3. Search-Intent Mix

Data and checks:

- Keyword data grouped as own-brand or registered-product brand/model, general category, competitor-related, attribute or use, and unexpected expansion.
- Product-level CPC, CVR, revenue, and ROAS for the groups or products associated with those contexts.
- Whether broad or ambiguous contexts repeatedly increase clicks without conversions.

Interpretation:

- Keyword data diagnoses the quality and breadth of traffic.
- Product/group data determines whether that traffic is worth paying for.
- Use low-bid exploratory groups when attribution is uncertain; do not claim exact keyword-level success.

#### 4. Seasonality

Data and checks:

- Weekly or monthly impressions, clicks, CPC, conversions, revenue, and ROAS by product.
- Current demand trend compared with the previous period and, where available, the same season last year.
- Stock, delivery capacity, event schedule, and expected peak period.
- Performance difference between seasonal and evergreen products.

Interpretation:

- Before the peak, secure learning and identify products capable of converting.
- During the peak, concentrate budget on proven seasonal products and monitor marginal ROAS.
- After the peak, reduce bids and budgets based on the speed of demand decline rather than stopping all seasonal products on one fixed date.

#### 5. Brand Strength

Data and checks:

- Share of clicks, conversions, revenue, and ROAS from own-brand or registered-product brand/model contexts.
- Conversion-rate and CPC differences between brand and general contexts.
- For wholesalers, whether registered brands and models create meaningful demand without implying that the seller owns those brands.
- Whether brand demand is concentrated on specific products.

Interpretation:

- Strong own-brand sellers can protect and expand proven brand-product combinations.
- Weak-brand sellers need product competitiveness and general-demand conversion before broad expansion.
- Wholesalers should analyze registered-product brand/model demand and product structure, not use own-brand framing.

#### 6. Product Competitiveness and Conversion Readiness

Data and checks:

- Product CTR where available, average CPC, clicks, CVR, revenue, and ROAS.
- Product title clarity and fit with the intended demand.
- Thumbnail clarity and visibility of product, configuration, size, or package.
- Price and per-unit or per-configuration competitiveness.
- Review and purchase signals as observed competitiveness indicators.
- Detail-page clarity, persuasion, and consistency with the clicked product.

Interpretation:

- Low clicks or weak CTR suggest a first-screen competitiveness issue such as title, thumbnail, price, or product selection.
- Sufficient clicks with weak CVR suggest price/package, review base, product-page persuasion, or demand mismatch.
- Strong CVR and ROAS with limited traffic support cautious bid or budget expansion.

### Analysis Output

For each advertiser, summarize:

1. Product structure diagnosis.
2. Product profitability boundary.
3. Search-context diagnosis from keyword data.
4. Seasonal demand stage.
5. Brand or registered-product brand/model influence.
6. Product competitiveness diagnosis.
7. Product decisions: scale, keep, observe, reduce, or stop.
8. Time and budget actions.

This keeps keyword data in its correct supporting role while making the product-centered report the operating and decision backbone.

## Outbound Pre-Diagnosis Without an Advertising Report

When an outbound seller has not shared advertising reports, do not imitate a performance audit. Build a light pre-diagnosis from observable evidence and connect it to what GabiaCNS would verify after data access.

### Evidence Levels

Separate every statement into one of three levels:

1. **Observed**
   - Visible in the minishop, product pages, search results, or supplied URLs.
   - Examples: product count, category mix, similar listings, seasonal products, title, thumbnail, price, package, reviews, registered brand or model.

2. **Operating hypothesis**
   - A plausible risk created by the visible product structure and current Gmarket environment.
   - Examples: budget may be dispersed across similar products; automatic expansion may broaden exposure; seasonal products may need budget concentration.
   - Use conditional language such as `가능성이 있습니다`, `확인이 필요합니다`, or `운영 중이라면 점검이 필요합니다`.

3. **Data verification item**
   - Cannot be concluded without the account report.
   - Examples: actual CPC, CVR, ROAS, hourly efficiency, profitable product, expanded-query contribution, group fragmentation, and current budget concentration.

Never turn an operating hypothesis into a confirmed account problem.

### What Can Be Analyzed Before Report Access

#### Product structure

- Count and categorize representative, best, popular, seasonal, evergreen, and similar products.
- Identify visible overlap in titles, thumbnails, configurations, and price ranges.
- Suggest a provisional core, test, and hold product structure.
- Do not claim that the actual ad groups are fragmented unless the account structure is known.

#### Product competitiveness

- Compare representative products with visible competing products on title clarity, thumbnail clarity, price, package size, reviews, and purchase signals.
- Identify which product appears most suitable as the initial advertising focus.
- Describe review or purchase volume as a visible competitiveness signal, not a seller-controlled ad lever.

#### Seasonality

- Determine whether currently emphasized products are entering, within, or leaving a likely demand period.
- Recommend preparing, concentrating, or tapering seasonal operation as a hypothesis.
- Do not describe the entire seller as seasonal based on only a few products.

#### Brand and demand structure

- For brand sellers, inspect own-brand product concentration and whether brand and general-demand products can be separated.
- For wholesalers, inspect registered-product brands, models, and the breadth of categories.
- Use competitor-related or general keywords only as search-context categories to review later, not as confirmed current performance drivers.

#### Margin proxy

- Selling price and package can be observed, but cost and contribution margin usually cannot.
- Do not estimate break-even ROAS without margin inputs.
- State that product-level profitability will be evaluated after confirming the seller's allowable advertising cost or target ROAS.

### Recommended Outbound Email Structure

1. **Why the operating structure matters**
   - Explain the shift from keyword-centered control to product-centered and automated expansion.

2. **Seller-specific observations**
   - Mention two or three visible facts from the current minishop or proposal-target products.

3. **Possible operating risks**
   - Present only conditional hypotheses related to product overlap, budget dispersion, seasonality, broad matching, or weak first-screen competitiveness.

4. **Three recommended checks**
   - Product and group simplification.
   - Product-level performance classification.
   - Product- and time-based bid or budget adjustment.

5. **GabiaCNS method**
   - Explain that GabiaCNS reports product-level cost, clicks, average CPC, conversions, CVR, revenue, and ROAS and translates them into maintain, expand, reduce, observe, or stop actions.

6. **Low-friction next step**
   - Offer a brief diagnostic after receiving a limited report extract rather than demanding full account access immediately.

### Minimum Data Request After Initial Interest

Ask for the smallest useful dataset:

- Recent four to eight weeks.
- Product name or product code.
- Cost.
- Clicks.
- Average CPC.
- Conversions.
- CVR.
- Conversion revenue.
- ROAS.
- Group name and hourly data if available.

Optional:

- Approximate target ROAS or margin range.
- Current priority products.
- Products the seller does not want to advertise.

This is enough to move from a pre-diagnosis to an initial product-centered action plan without requiring a full account handover.

Explain the progression:

Connect the change to the seller's product structure instead of repeating only a generic platform explanation.

Do not use a generic heading such as `공통 운영 방향` in a seller-specific email. It reads like copied template text.

- product-level performance decisions;
- product- and time-based bid control;

Include these conservatively:

Each outbound email should:

- contain seller-specific observations that could not be pasted unchanged into another seller's email;
- separate observations, hypotheses, and future data verification;
- explain why product-level reporting matters under automatic expansion;

- end with a low-friction request for a limited recent product-level report rather than immediate full account access.

### Seller-Archetype Branching for Minishop-Based Outbound

When producing many outbound emails, classify the visible seller structure before drafting. Use the classification only to choose the diagnostic lens; the final email must still cite seller-specific products and configurations.

- **Seasonal fresh-food specialist**
  - Check short selling windows, inventory volatility, weight/size duplication, packaging, and the timing of budget concentration and reduction.
- **Single-product or very narrow seller**
  - Budget dispersion is not the main risk. Focus on the representative product's title, thumbnail, price, options, review signals, conversion readiness, and expansion timing.
- **Own-brand multi-category seller**
  - Select brand-representative products by category. Separate brand demand from general function or use demand and avoid spreading launch budgets across every category.
- **Similar-SKU specialist**
  - Typical examples include masks, bedding, supplements, condoms, toothpaste, and power strips. Select representative configurations and compare pack size, color, model, feature, and unit economics to reduce internal paid-click competition.
- **Brand/model electronics seller**
  - Separate brand/model search context from general category and seasonal demand. Select representative models and do not run every color or near-identical model at equal strength.
- **Used or one-off inventory seller**
  - Connect advertising to live inventory, item condition, delivery/install range, and rapid stop or replacement after sale. Long stable learning assumptions may not apply.
- **High-SKU general or wholesale seller**
  - Product selection and group simplification come first. Prevent many products from accumulating small, inconclusive spend and prioritize profitable or representative product clusters.

For all archetypes, keep the same data hierarchy:

1. Public minishop evidence for the outbound pre-diagnosis.
2. Keyword data for search-context interpretation.
3. Product-level reporting for maintain, scale, observe, reduce, or stop decisions.
4. Margin and time-of-day data for profitability protection.

## Full Minishop Commercial Audit for SME Sellers

SME sellers require a more detailed product audit because they often have limited budgets, weak brand demand, overlapping listings, shared supplier content, and insufficient differentiation. Do not move directly from a minishop URL to an advertising recommendation.

### Analysis Sequence

#### 1. Catalog Census

- Inspect the full active catalog when technically accessible, not only the five best products.
- Record category, product name, brand, model, form, capacity, size, pack quantity, price, shipping condition, visible review/purchase signals, and stock or sale status.
- Separate currently active products from old, duplicated, sold-out, or practically dormant listings.
- Use best products, popularity-sorted products, and currently emphasized products to understand where demand is concentrated.

#### 2. Product-Family Clustering

Group listings that satisfy the same purchase need:

- Same underlying product with 1-unit, 2-unit, 4-unit, or other bundle variations.
- Same model with different colors or installation options.
- Same item with minor title or thumbnail differences.
- Similar products likely to compete for the same general and expanded search demand.

Do not count each listing as a genuinely different advertising opportunity.

#### 3. Bundle-Role Selection

Do not automatically advertise every bundle. Also do not automatically keep only one.

- Single unit: lower entry price and first-purchase role, but potentially weak margin.
- Mid bundle: often balances conversion rate, order value, and shipping economics.
- Large bundle: higher order value and allowable ad cost, but potentially lower conversion rate.

Default selection:

- Choose one representative bundle when purchase intent and economics are nearly identical.
- Keep up to two bundles only when they have clearly different roles, such as acquisition versus profitability.
- Compare unit price, total price, review concentration, conversion readiness, shipping economics, and expected margin before choosing.

#### 4. Standardized Competitor Comparison

Price competitiveness must not be judged from the displayed price alone. For each proposed general or attribute keyword, compare a bounded set of visible competing products under equivalent conditions:

- Same product type and intended use.
- Same or normalized capacity, count, size, or model.
- Final payable price where visible.
- Unit price.
- Shipping fee and minimum purchase requirement.
- Coupon or event condition where visible.
- Official, genuine, compatible, refurbished, used, or generic status.
- Review and purchase signals.
- Seller trust such as official partner or authorized seller status.

The conclusion should distinguish:

- lowest displayed price;
- lowest normalized unit price;
- strongest offer after shipping and bundle conditions;
- strongest trust or review proposition.

#### 5. Advertising-Candidate Scoring

Score products as:

- **Core:** clear price, configuration, trust, review, margin, or conversion-readiness advantage.
- **Efficiency:** limited volume but likely to protect ROAS.
- **Growth:** can compete in general demand if given controlled budget.
- **Test:** uncertain but worth a bounded experiment.
- **Hold:** insufficient differentiation, weak economics, duplicated demand, or poor product readiness.

Advertising candidates should be selected from product families, not raw listing count.

#### 6. Keyword and Group Design

Create groups only when the operating purpose differs:

- brand or registered-product brand/model;
- exact product form;
- general category;
- capacity, specification, function, or use;
- compatible or replacement demand;
- controlled exploratory expansion.

The group is a manageable observation window, not proof that expanded traffic is perfectly isolated.

#### 7. Output

For each minishop, provide:

1. Main and subcategory diagnosis.
2. Full catalog and active product-family summary.
3. Duplicate and bundle clusters.
4. Representative, test, and hold product shortlist.
5. Competitor-price comparison by proposed demand context.
6. Product-page and first-screen competitiveness findings.
7. Recommended group and bidding structure.
8. Items that cannot be confirmed without product-level advertising reports.

### Critical Cautions

- A full catalog scan does not mean every listed product is commercially meaningful.
- A lower price is not automatically a better advertising product if margin, reviews, shipping, or product-page readiness are weak.
- Best products can reflect historical sales rather than current advertising opportunity.
- Competitor comparisons must normalize bundle size, capacity, shipping, coupon, official/compatible status, and model condition.
- Do not state that large companies hold most advertising spend while SMEs hold most advertiser participation as a confirmed market fact unless supported by current evidence. It can be used as an internal business hypothesis, not an externally cited fact without a source.

## 2026-06-25 Learning: Search Placement Interpretation And Beauty Advertiser Operation

- The Kocoslab minishop is not an AGE LAB-only shop. Its visible best and popular products mix AGE LAB skincare, May Island skincare, and Dr. Haiyan whitening toothpaste.
- The visible popular assortment includes ampoule, sunscreen, cream, eye cream, masks, cleanser, hair care, and toothpaste. Category and purchase intent differ materially.

- This confirms that a product can hold organic or recommendation visibility while paid modules exist elsewhere on the same result page. Paid expansion should protect and complement that product asset rather than treating every visible rank as a bid-controlled position.

### 3. Beauty Advertiser Diagnosis With KRW 1M-3M Monthly Budget

- Do not assume the brand keyword is automatically the strongest traffic source. Brand search volume, conversion rate, and expanded-query contamination must be checked.
- Do not attribute poor brand-keyword control solely to automatic expansion without account evidence. Other possible causes include mixed product groups, multiple brands in one operating unit, bid differences, personalization, and product-quality differences.
- A KRW 1M-3M budget is generally too small to operate every listing across a broad multi-brand catalog with meaningful learning volume.
- Select advertising candidates by product family, not listing count. Multiple bundle sizes or nearly identical listings should normally share one representative acquisition product and, only when justified, one profitability-oriented bundle.

### 4. Recommended Manageable Observation Windows

Use a small number of operating zones:

1. **Brand and high-intent zone:** AGE LAB and AGE LAB plus product-type combinations. Protect likely efficient demand, but judge product-level cost, conversion, and ROAS rather than assuming keyword isolation.
2. **Product-problem and attribute zone:** blemish ampoule, whitening or tone-up sunscreen, snail cream, moisture or elasticity products. Keep only product and query combinations with clear relevance.
3. **Controlled general-demand zone:** broad terms such as ampoule, sunscreen, cream, or mask pack. Use only representative products with strong price, review, purchase, and product-page readiness.
4. **Separate category zone:** toothpaste and hair-care products should not share the same operational interpretation as facial skincare.

These zones are manageable observation windows, not proof that expanded search traffic is perfectly separated.

### 5. Budget Operating Logic

- Around KRW 1M: concentrate on two or three representative skincare products and one clearly separate test product. Minimize broad-category exposure.
- Around KRW 2M: maintain the core products and add one controlled attribute or general-demand test cluster.
- Around KRW 3M: add limited recommendation or automated-expansion coverage, but continue excluding duplicate bundles and low-readiness products from the core budget.
- Final allocation requires recent product-level cost, clicks, CPC, conversions, revenue, ROAS, margin, and stock information.

### 6. Inbound Call Guidance

- Start with the confirmed public observation: the shop has multiple brands and different product intents, and at least one AGE LAB product has visible organic and popularity signals.
- Explain the official placement structure: Power Click can appear in several search-result modules, while organic and recommendation exposure can coexist.
- Present automatic expansion as a reason to change the measurement unit from keyword rank to product performance, not as a claim that keywords have become meaningless.
- Ask for a recent four-to-eight-week product report and current campaign-group structure before diagnosing the exact cause of brand-keyword instability.
- Offer a first restructuring test around representative products, duplicate-bundle cleanup, and a small number of operating zones.

## 2026-06-25 Cross-Thread Learning: Repeat-Purchase And Seasonal Product Reasoning

### 1. Newly Learned Facts

### 2. New Facts To Apply

- AI Product Ads is a CPC performance ad that uses product data to expose products automatically across search and recommendation areas to customers with higher purchase probability.
- Campaign types remain threefold:
  - Integrated Operation: automatically finds sale-potential products and optimizes placements/bids.
  - Focused Operation: seller selects products; no keyword registration needed; suited to main products and sales reinforcement.
  - Direct Operation: seller can set keywords and bids more strategically and choose exposure areas.
- AI Product Ads structure is campaign -> group -> product.
- Campaign-level settings: campaign type, campaign name, campaign period.
- Group-level settings vary by campaign type:
  - Integrated: group name, daily budget, excluded products after initial setup/editing.
  - Focused: group name, ad products, bidding method, bid, daily budget.
  - Direct: group name, ad products, exposure area, daily budget.
- Product-level settings are available in Direct Operation: keywords, keyword bids, product-level bids.
- Ad management allows campaign/group/product-level performance checks and setting changes.
- The ad management date range can be queried up to 62 days at a time.
- Ad management summary compares the selected period with the immediately preceding period of the same length.
- An AI product recommendation area can recommend up to 20 high-potential products per day that are not currently advertised.
- If all 20 recommended products are reviewed/added, a new list can appear the next day. If no recommendable product exists, the module is not shown.
- If all products are already advertised and no Integrated Operation campaign is running, an Integrated Operation campaign recommendation module can appear; if Integrated Operation is already running, no recommendation module appears.
- Operation status download can export currently active campaigns, groups, and products to Excel.

### 3. Report Metric Corrections

- Operational metrics:
  - impressions: actual ad exposure count; repeated exposures on the same screen are deduplicated.
  - clicks: valid billable clicks.
  - CTR: clicks / impressions x 100.
  - average CPC: ad cost / clicks; VAT included.
  - ad cost: actual ad spend including seller deposit and e-money; VAT included; guide expresses cost as click cost x 1.1.
- Seller-level conversion metrics include not only the clicked ad product but also other products from the same seller purchased or added to cart after the ad click.
- Seller-level attribution window: purchase or add-to-cart must occur within 15 days after ad click.
- Seller conversion count: total purchase orders after ad click, including other products from same seller.
- Ad conversion quantity: total product quantity sold after ad click, including other seller products; option quantity excluded from quantity count based on main product.
- Seller conversion amount: total purchase amount after ad click, including main product, option product, and add-on product purchase amounts; discount, coupon discount, and shipping fee excluded.
- ROAS: seller conversion amount / ad cost x 100.
- CVR: seller conversion count / clicks x 100.
- Seller add-to-cart quantity includes all seller products added to cart within 15 days after ad click.
- Ad-product-level conversion metrics include only the clicked ad product, not other products from the same seller.
- Ad product conversion count: purchase count of the clicked ad product only.
- Ad product conversion quantity: sold quantity of the clicked ad product only; main-product basis, excluding option quantity.
- Ad product conversion amount: purchase amount from the clicked ad product; includes main, option, and add-on product purchase amounts; excludes discount, coupon discount, and shipping fee.
- Ad product CVR: ad product conversion count / clicks x 100.
- Ad product add-to-cart quantity: add-to-cart quantity for the clicked ad product within 15 days.

### 4. Strategic Interpretation For Reports

- Do not treat seller-level ROAS and ad-product-level ROAS as the same thing.
- Seller-level conversion answers: did the ad click contribute to this seller's total orders/revenue, including cross-sell to other products?
- Ad-product-level conversion answers: did the clicked product itself sell after the click?
- If seller-level conversion is high but ad-product-level conversion is low, the advertised product may be a traffic-entry product, while another product closes the sale.
- If ad-product-level conversion is high, the advertised product itself has direct purchase competitiveness and can be a Focused Operation or Direct Operation candidate.
- If add-to-cart is high but purchase is low, check price, coupon/discount, shipping fee, delivery schedule, reviews, option structure, and purchase friction.
- If impressions are high but CTR is low, check product title, thumbnail, price, visible benefit, and category/search fit.
- If clicks are high but CVR is low, check detail page, price/package, shipping, reviews, options, stock, and product competitiveness.

## 2026-07-16 Field Learning: Direct Operation Migration, Keyword Attribution, And CBT Delivery Volatility

### 1. Newly Confirmed Fact And Field Observations

- Official guide checked on 2026-07-16: https://ads.esmplus.com/ads-guide-report-view
- The keyword-level report is defined as performance by `campaign > group > product > registered keyword`. The public guide does not identify it as an actual user search-query report.

  - some migrated Direct Operation groups show old low-volume long-tail keywords receiving impression/click volume comparable to former core keywords;
  - Gmarket recommended bids can be materially lower than legacy Power Click bid ranges;
  - some accounts' initially fast budget depletion later stabilized;
  - some products with weak exposure began receiving impressions after `search and recommendation` exposure was enabled;
  - participation expanded during the CBT period, so auction and delivery conditions are still moving.

### 2. Corrections To Existing Interpretation

- Direct Operation resembles old Power Click in its keyword and bid controls, but it should not be treated as the same delivery logic. Registered keywords can expand to related keywords, exposure is personalized, and spacing variants can affect matching.
- `Long-tail keyword as a trigger for the core keyword` is currently an unverified hypothesis. Removing that keyword may simply move expanded traffic to another registered keyword, change the auction path, or reduce delivery without restoring the former core-keyword pattern.
- Enabling recommendation exposure and then seeing more impressions does not prove search exposure recovered. It may only mean new internal recommendation inventory was added. Placement-level evidence is required before attributing the change.
- Stabilized spend after an early surge is consistent with learning or pacing stabilization, but it does not prove either mechanism. Treat it as an observation until Gmarket confirms the cause.

### 3. Product-Centered Practical Principles

1. Do not migrate every historical keyword merely because it existed in Power Click. Remove stale, obsolete, duplicated, and role-less long-tail keywords before migration, or rebuild the Direct campaign when the legacy structure is noisy.
2. Use Direct Operation as a controlled observation layer, not as a promise of exact keyword control. Register a compact set of representative intent keywords per product instead of every spacing and long-tail variation.
3. Treat the platform recommended bid and legacy bid as two different reference points:
   - start exploratory and low-priority keywords near the recommended range;
   - use legacy CPC and conversion history only as a guardrail for proven core demand;
   - avoid applying the full legacy bid to expanded inventory without direct-product conversion evidence.
4. Change one major variable at a time and hold the structure long enough to observe it. Separate bid changes, keyword removal, recommendation-area activation, product changes, and budget changes in the operating log.
5. Read keyword-level performance together with product-level direct conversion. A registered keyword can receive attributed traffic through expansion, while direct-product metrics determine whether the advertised product itself merits more budget.
6. When search-only versus search-and-recommendation performance cannot be separated in the report, use sequential time-boxed tests and state the attribution limitation explicitly.

- A scope note such as `이번 모니터링 대상 키워드와 수집 시점 기준` should remain close to the comparison table.
- Use concrete product examples before discussing platform reform or agency capabilities.

### 2. Critical Corrections

- Do not conclude that `the product is competitive and only the advertising strategy is the problem` from purchase-quantity growth alone. Product competitiveness still requires price/package, click-through, direct conversion, margin, review, delivery, and landing evidence.
- Do not call every search term where a competitor appears a missed keyword opportunity. First confirm that the seller actually has a product matching the same use, material, quantity, specification, or customer purpose.
- A competitor with a broader catalog can naturally appear across more search purposes. This is a product-portfolio difference before it is an advertising-management difference.
- A product not found in one monitored ranking table is `not observed within the checked keyword, time, and collection scope`; it is not proof of platform-wide non-delivery.
- Do not claim that the gap will definitely widen after reform. AI automation can widen the gap for weak or unclear product information, but it can also discover additional demand for a well-structured product. Present this as an operating risk and test question, not a guaranteed outcome.
- Do not promise competitor-keyword harvesting, competitor-brand groups, keyword scrubbing, exact rank recovery, or exclusion of expanded terms. These are either poorly attributable, weakly controllable, or not central to Integrated/Focused Operation.
- Do not present DIAD time-of-day bid adjustment as an AI Product Ads capability unless integration and supported controls are currently confirmed. In the new center, campaign-type controls differ, and schedule/bulk-edit support can be limited.

### 3. Campaign-Type Interpretation

- `통합 운영형`: the seller does not register keywords or choose individual products. Use competitor monitoring to identify products or product information that should be excluded, improved, or evaluated after AI selects them, not to prescribe missing-keyword registration.
- `집중 운영형`: the seller selects focus products while AI handles delivery. Use observed search-contact gaps to decide which representative products deserve focused learning and whether their title, category, attributes, price/package, and thumbnail clearly express the intended demand.
- `직접 운영형`: keywords and bids can be set, but registered keywords may also expand to related searches. Use a compact set of representative intent keywords to create a manageable observation window; do not rebuild an exhaustive legacy keyword list.
- Public competitor/search monitoring is a `market-demand and product-readiness input`. Product impressions, clicks, average CPC, direct conversion, direct revenue, and direct ROAS are the operating decision data.

### 4. Recommended Outbound Logic

1. Show the seller's named product and dated purchase-quantity change.
2. Compare only genuinely equivalent or adjacent competitor products and state the monitored scope.
3. Explain the gap as an observed `purchase-purpose contact-point difference`, not complete keyword coverage.
4. Separate three possible causes:
   - no matching seller product,
   - matching product exists but product information or offer is weak,
   - matching product exists but was not observed in the monitored search contact point.
5. Translate the finding into campaign roles:
   - broad portfolio discovery through Integrated Operation,
   - representative winning products through Focused Operation,
   - limited intent hypotheses through Direct Operation.
6. State that the first operating decision will be made from product-level direct performance, while seller-level conversion is kept as cross-sell contribution.
7. Close with GabiaCNS's monitoring, product-centered report, budget reallocation, and campaign-role review rather than a promise to discover and register endless keywords.

## 2026-07-21 Learning: Mini-Shop And Product Diagnosis Must Drive AI Product Ads Assignment

### 1. What The PeopleB Case Does Well

- Dated purchase-quantity changes, named products, and market-level purchase-keyword evidence make the proposal feel researched.
- Catalog breadth, representative products, competitor products, and observed search contact points are all useful evidence inputs.
- The strongest underlying conclusion is that a seller with a broad catalog needs representative-product selection more than indiscriminate product or keyword expansion.

### 2. Critical Corrections

- A mini-shop analysis is not complete when it only counts products by feature. It must explain product clusters, duplicate bundles or variants, price ladders, representative SKUs, low-readiness products, and the customer's purchase purpose for each cluster.
- A higher average selling price does not prove premium positioning unless product type, function, installation method, package, and brand are normalized. Compare like-for-like products before calling the seller premium.
- A monitored keyword where the seller was not observed is not automatically a missed keyword opportunity. It can reflect collection timing, personalized delivery, rank movement, or product mismatch.
- Under AI Product Ads, Focused and Integrated Operation are not keyword-registration structures. Do not promise keyword-by-keyword expansion, exclusion, budget allocation, or bid adjustment in those campaigns.
- Daily budgets are managed at group level, not as a native per-product budget. Separate groups only when product role, bid strategy, margin, or evaluation rule genuinely differs.
- Competitor-brand tests should not become the central strategy. Use them only as limited Direct Operation hypotheses when the seller carries the brand or has a genuinely comparable substitute. Evaluate continuation by product-level direct performance.
- Public purchase-quantity growth is product-market evidence, not proof of ad-attributed conversions or profitability.

### 3. Required Mini-Shop And Product Analysis For Future Outbound Mail

For every seller-specific proposal, check and explain:

1. Catalog structure: main categories, feature/use clusters, bundle/variant overlap, accessories versus complete products.
2. Hero SKU evidence: cumulative purchase quantity, recent change, review concentration, price/package, and whether the current mini-shop placement supports that role.
3. Product readiness: title, thumbnail, visible price/package, category and attributes, stock, shipping or installation conditions, warranty/AS, and detail-page persuasion.
4. Like-for-like competition: compare products with the same use, specification, installation method, and price range before drawing a competitive conclusion.
5. Product role assignment: Focused priority product, Direct hypothesis product, Integrated discovery product, or hold/exclude.

### 4. AI Product Ads Campaign Assignment

- Focused Operation: use for proven or promising representative SKUs. The seller selects products, while operation is managed through group-level automatic bidding or maximum CPC and daily budget. Do not describe it as a keyword group.
- Direct Operation: use for a compact set of clear purchase-intent hypotheses such as product model, installation environment, door type, or specific function. Avoid rebuilding the full legacy keyword list because related-search expansion and limited bulk management reduce exact control.
- Integrated Operation: use after catalog cleanup for broader discovery among non-hero products. Exclude or hold duplicate bundles, low-stock items, low-margin accessories, and products with weak information readiness before broad expansion.
- Hold/Exclude: keep products out of the initial learning set when their role, offer, stock, or product information is not ready.

Assign one primary role per product by default. Do not duplicate the same product across campaign types without a time-boxed hypothesis, separate budgets, and clear success criteria.

### 5. Initial Operating Sequence

1. Before launch: classify the full active catalog and remove duplicate or role-less products from the initial test pool.
2. Week 1: launch a small set of representative products with stable budget and bid conditions; monitor spend velocity, impressions, clicks, CTR, and average CPC without changing multiple variables at once.
3. Week 2: compare seller-level conversion with ad-product-level direct conversion, direct revenue, direct CVR, add-to-cart, and ROAS.
4. First adjustment:
   - expand products with both strong seller contribution and direct-product conversion;
   - keep entry products when seller conversion is strong but direct conversion is weak and cross-sell is meaningful;
   - revise title, thumbnail, offer, or detail page when impressions/clicks exist but direct conversion is weak;
   - reduce or hold products with weak direct conversion and no cross-sell contribution.
5. Only after the first learning window should Integrated discovery or additional Direct hypotheses be widened.

### 6. Better Outbound Story Order

Use this order for future purchase-quantity-based seller mail:

1. What was checked in the mini-shop and why the product structure matters.
2. Which representative products are actually moving and how comparable competitors differ.
3. What the current product setup may be missing: overlap, unclear product role, offer gap, or search-purpose mismatch.
4. Which products should go to Focused, Direct, Integrated, or Hold, and why.
5. What will be observed during the first one to two weeks and what decision follows each result.
6. What GabiaCNS will deliver: product-role map, migration/rebuild recommendation, product-centered report, change log, and budget reallocation actions.

## 2026-07-22 Learning: Product Evidence Continuity, Role Readiness, And Supporting Signals

### 1) 새로 학습한 사실

- 제공 파일 기반 사실: 서로 다른 날짜의 판매수량 수집본은 `동일 상품번호`가 두 시점에 모두 존재할 때만 증감값을 계산할 수 있다. 뒤 시점에 처음 포착된 상품은 `증가`가 아니라 `누적 판매수량 신규 포착`, 뒤 시점에 보이지 않는 상품은 `판매 0건`이나 `판매 감소`가 아니라 `해당 수집 범위에서 미포착`으로 구분해야 한다.
- 제공 파일 기반 사례: 2026-07-09와 2026-07-15 수집본을 비교한 아웃바운드 작업에서 덤n덤은 대표 변기시트 3종이 신규 포착됐고, 든든스토어는 청소밀대와 현관 방충망만 수집 범위에 포함됐으며, 쇼핑천재는 동일 상품번호 기준으로 레오박스 `+8`, 다용도 바구니 `+31`이 확인됐다. 이 값은 해당 파일·기간·상품번호에 한정된 관찰값이다.
- 제공 파일 기반 사례: 제이숲 헤어팩·트리트먼트 전환 키워드 자료의 `타 브랜드 추정 911개/전체 1,324개`는 서로 다른 키워드 문구의 종류 수다. 전환 건수, 검색량, 매출 기여도 또는 시장점유율 비중으로 해석할 수 없다.
- 제공 파일 기반 사례: 제이숲은 등록 소카테고리만 보면 헤어팩·트리트먼트 관련 상품이 17개였지만, 상품명과 세트 구성을 함께 확인하면 다른 소카테고리에 등록된 관련 상품 32개를 포함해 49개가 확인됐다. 카테고리 코드만으로 광고 후보를 뽑으면 관련 묶음상품과 내부 중복을 놓칠 수 있다.
- 공식 확인(2026-07-22): G마켓 AI Product Ads 공식 소개는 상품정보, 고객 관심도·구매 의도, 실시간 성과 등에 따라 검색·추천 영역의 노출이 유연하게 달라질 수 있다고 안내한다. 공식 공개 가이드에서는 `스타배송 여부가 광고 순위나 AI 상품 선정에 직접 가점을 준다`는 규칙을 확인하지 못했다.

### 2) 기존 지식에서 수정할 점

- 대표상품 선정은 `AI가 선호할 상품`이라는 단일 기준으로 설명하지 않는다. 먼저 핵심 매출, 신규 유입, 객단가 확대, 탐색, 보류 등 `사업상 역할`을 정하고, 그다음 상품정보 적합성, 가격·구성, 판매 흐름, 리뷰, 재고·배송, 상세페이지, 수익성 등 `광고 운영 전 상품 점검` 항목을 별도 축으로 평가한다.
- 스타배송은 판매량 상위 상품에서 함께 관찰될 수 있는 보조 신호이지, 대표상품 선정의 필수조건이나 성과의 원인으로 두지 않는다. 인기상품이 스타배송을 채택했을 가능성 등 역인과관계도 남아 있다.
- 경쟁사 브랜드 키워드 종류가 많다는 사실만으로 직접 운영형 확대를 정당화하지 않는다. 키워드별 전환 규모가 없는 자료라면 `수요 표현의 다양성`까지만 말하고, 상품 적합성과 효율 구간이 확인된 `브랜드+상품유형` 조합을 제한적으로 검증한다.
- 공개 검색·크롤링에서 여러 번 보인 상품을 `노출률이 높은 상품`으로 일반화하지 않는다. 동일 검색어·기기·시점·수집 방식에서의 `관찰 노출률` 또는 `반복 포착률`로만 표현한다.

### 3) 실무 적용 원칙

1. 판매수량 증감표는 `상품번호 × 기준일`로 정규화하고, `동일 상품번호 비교`, `신규 포착`, `미포착`을 서로 다른 상태값으로 저장한다.
2. 상품 선정표는 두 축으로 만든다.
   - 사업상 역할: 핵심 매출 / 입문·유입 / 객단가 확대 / 탐색 / 보류
   - 광고 운영 전 상품 점검: 상품명·카테고리·속성 / 가격·구성 / 판매·리뷰 / 재고·배송 / 상세페이지 / 수익성
3. 카테고리 분석은 등록 소카테고리와 함께 상품명, 옵션, 증정·묶음 구성을 교차 확인한다. 유사 SKU가 많으면 모든 상품을 동시에 학습시키지 말고 대표 구성을 먼저 정한다.
4. 공개 모니터링은 `관찰 노출률`, `대표상품 집중도`, `자사 상품 간 중복 포착률`, `상품정보 적합도 차이`를 진단용으로 사용한다. 최종 확대·감액은 계정 리포트의 상품별 노출, 클릭, 평균 CPC, 직접 전환, 직접 매출, 직접 ROAS와 판매자 전환을 함께 보고 결정한다.
5. 배송 경쟁력은 가격·구성·리뷰·판매 흐름·상품정보와 함께 평가하되, 공식 근거가 없는 순위 가점이나 인과효과는 제안서에 쓰지 않는다.

### 3. AI Product Ads 캠페인 역할 수정

- `통합 운영형에 후보 상품을 넣는다`고 표현하지 않는다. 통합 운영형은 판매자가 상품을 직접 선택하는 구조가 아니라, 판매아이디의 전체 판매가능 상품 중 AI가 적합한 상품을 자동 선별한다.
- 통합 운영형은 입찰가를 설정하지 않고 일예산을 설정한다. 운영 후 제외 상품을 관리할 수 있으므로, 상품 수가 많거나 유사 구성이 많은 판매자는 핵심·육성·직접 테스트 상품과 상품정보 재등록 검토상품을 제외한 나머지 범위에서 자동 탐색 역할을 맡기는 방식을 검토한다.
- 통합 운영형 자체가 항상 보조 캠페인인 것은 아니다. 상품 구성이 단순하고 전체 상품을 넓게 자동 운영하려는 판매자에게는 주력 운영형이 될 수 있다. 다만 제이숲처럼 카테고리와 유사 구성이 많고 상품별 역할을 구분해야 하는 판매자에게는 통제 가능한 집중·직접 운영형을 먼저 두고 통합 운영형을 탐색 보완용으로 쓰는 편이 합리적이다.
- 집중 운영형은 판매자가 상품을 선택하고 `자동 입찰` 또는 `최대 입찰가`를 설정한다. 직접 운영형은 상품, 키워드, 키워드별 입찰가를 설정하며 검색·추천 영역을 함께 선택하면 상품별 입찰가도 설정한다.
- `보조상품은 직접 운영형`이라는 일괄 규칙은 사용하지 않는다. 회사가 성장시키려는 상품은 `육성상품`으로 별도 분류하고, 넓은 관련 수요 학습이 목적이면 집중 운영형, 특정 기능·구성·브랜드 수요 확인이 목적이면 직접 운영형을 선택한다.

### 4. 초기 입찰·예산 기준은 공식 정책과 운영 가설을 분리

- G마켓 공식 가이드는 집중 운영형의 최대 입찰가와 직접 운영형의 키워드·상품별 입찰가 입력 범위를 안내하지만, 핵심상품 대비 육성상품의 적정 비율을 공식 권장하지 않는다.

### 7. 판매자 전체 성과와 광고 상품 직접 성과를 함께 보는 이유

- 판매자 기준 전환은 광고 클릭 상품이 아닌 같은 판매자의 다른 상품 구매도 포함하므로 광고의 전체 매출 기여도를 보여준다.
- 광고 상품 기준 전환은 클릭한 상품 자체의 구매만 포함하므로 어떤 상품에 광고비를 계속 배분할지 판단하는 데 필요하다.
- 판매자 ROAS가 높고 광고 상품 직접 ROAS가 낮다면 해당 상품이 다른 상품 구매를 유도하는 입문 상품일 수 있다. 반대로 다른 상품 구매가 우연히 귀속된 것일 수도 있으므로 직접 성과를 함께 보지 않으면 약한 상품에 광고비를 계속 배분할 위험이 있다.
- 두 지표가 모두 높으면 핵심 확대 상품, 판매자 지표만 높으면 유입 후 다른 상품 구매 기여를 확인할 상품, 직접 지표만 높으면 해당 상품 자체의 전환은 좋지만 교차구매가 적은 상품, 두 지표가 모두 낮으면 감액·중단 검토상품으로 분류한다.

## 2026-07-27 판매자 상품과 자사 브랜드 구분, 표본 선정과 예산 범위 보완

### 1) 새로 학습한 사실

- 제공 파일 기반 사실: 홈앤미 판매 상품 노출에는 홈앤미 자체 브랜드 상품과 홈앤미가 공식 판매하는 고로고로 브랜드 상품이 함께 포함됐다. 판매자 등록 상품 기준의 노출 집중도는 유효하지만 이를 홈앤미 자체 브랜드의 노출 강점으로 해석할 수는 없다.
- 제공 파일 기반 사실: 전체 판매 인기상품과 상세 운영 표본 상품은 다를 수 있다. 홈앤미 제안에서는 판매순위만이 아니라 G마켓 전환 키워드와 네이버 쇼핑 교차분석이 가능한 테이프클리너를 시장 표본으로 사용했다.
- 실무 판단: 예산 비중을 범위로 제시하면 각 범위의 중간값이나 상·하한 합계가 100%가 아닐 수 있다. 실제 집행안에는 합계를 100%로 맞추는 고정 원칙과 조정 순서를 함께 적어야 한다.

### 2) 기존 지식에서 수정할 점

- `판매자 계정에 등록된 상품`을 모두 `판매자 자체 브랜드 상품`으로 묶지 않는다. 자체 브랜드, 공식 유통 브랜드, 기타 판매 상품을 분리하고 상품번호별 소유·유통 관계를 확인한다.
- 판매순위 1위를 자동으로 제안서의 대표 분석 상품으로 선택하지 않는다. 표본 선정 이유가 시장성, 자료 연속성, 광고상품 적합성 또는 검증 목적이라면 본문에서 그 이유를 명시한다.
- 예산 범위만 나열하고 실제 100% 조정 규칙을 생략하지 않는다.

### 3) 실무 적용 원칙

1. 상품 원본에 `판매자`, `자체 브랜드`, `제조·브랜드`, `공식 유통 여부`, `상품번호` 필드를 둔다.

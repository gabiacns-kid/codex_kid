# Kakao ShoppingHow Reference

Use this reference when the user asks about Kakao ShoppingHow, Daum ShoppingHow, shopping mall listing, shopping comparison exposure, SHOP ads, CPC/CPS listing, or shopping-only entry.

## Core Positioning

ShoppingHow is separate from Kakao Moment and separate from the Kakao Commerce Ad Center. It is a shopping mall/product listing and shopping ad route that exposes advertiser products through ShoppingHow, Daum integrated search, product search, price comparison, KakaoTalk, Daum, Nate, and related shopping channels depending on entry type and product data.

Do not describe ShoppingHow as ordinary Kakao Moment display advertising. Also do not confuse it with Kakao Commerce seller ads for Gift, TokDeal, or KakaoTalk Shopping Tab.

## Entry Types

ShoppingHow has three entry routes:

| Type | Billing | Best fit | Key condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| CPC entry | Cost per product click | Small and mid-size shopping malls | Product DB/EP linked; no entry fee; product click incurs fee based on product price tier |
| CPS entry | Cost per sale | Mid-to-large shopping malls | Written CPS contract; sale commission on completed sales; suitable for large product DB, commonly 5,000+ products |
| Ad-only entry | Ad fee for thumbnail ad use | Advertisers that only want thumbnail ads | No separate product DB registration; for ad-only shopping promotion |

If a shopping mall has CPC or CPS entry, it does not need separate ad-only entry to use ad-only products.

## CPC Notes

- CPC entry charges only when a user clicks a listed product and moves to the advertiser site.
- Official CPC examples by product price tier include KRW 20-70 per click, and shopping mall-name only exposure is listed separately.
- ShoppingHow applies invalid-click filtering. A single user click on one product is charged only once per hour, and suspected invalid software/robot clicks are removed.
- CPC entry after approval generally proceeds through product EP linkage, product info generation, Shopping Cash charging, product mapping, and product exposure.

## CPS Notes

- CPS entry charges a percentage of completed sales.
- Official guidance describes CPS as suitable for mid-to-large shopping malls and states that CPS is handled through a written contract with a ShoppingHow representative.
- CPS after contract/approval proceeds through product EP linkage, product info generation, product mapping, and product exposure.
- CPS support may include product DB exposure, planning events, main banners, quick links, and other ShoppingHow inventory depending on contract/type.

## Advertising Workflow

SHOP ad execution generally follows:

1. Entry application and completion
2. Shopping Cash charge
3. Ad purchase
4. Creative registration
5. Creative review
6. Ad execution
7. Ad management

Official Kakao help states that ad registration and management can be supported by partnership agencies officially contracted with Kakao. Therefore, do not say "ShoppingHow ads cannot be handled by an agency" without checking the exact product/account status.

## Product Data

- ShoppingHow product exposure depends on product data collected as EP.
- EP collection can include full EP and summary EP; full EP once per day is recommended in official help.
- Product mapping and exposure can fail if entry approval, EP linkage, product info generation, or mapping is incomplete.

## Proposal Guidance

Before recommending ShoppingHow, check:

- Does the advertiser operate an actual ecommerce mall?
- Is the objective product sale, product comparison exposure, or simple display/thumbnail advertising?
- Can the advertiser provide and maintain product EP data?
- Is the product volume closer to CPC entry, CPS entry, or ad-only entry?
- Can the advertiser prepare business/member account, entry documents, shopping cash/payment, and product/creative review assets?
- If the user is asking about Kakao Shopping ads, first separate ShoppingHow from Kakao Commerce Ad Center and Kakao Moment product catalog ads.

## Gmarket Product-Centered Operation

Use for Gmarket/Auction reports, open-market seller proposals, or AI Product Ads discussions.

- Treat Gmarket ads as product-centered first, not manually controlled keyword campaigns.
- Keywords are useful diagnostic context, not always the primary control lever.
- Product matching may expand automatically from the registered product.
- If keyword exclusions are not available in the actual account/UI, do not propose exclusion keywords.
- If exposure is personalized, do not overstate fixed rank management.
- Weekly actions should focus on product bid strength, product keep/observe/reduce decisions, and product competitiveness checks.

Weekly insight order:

1. Summarize market/account-level performance.
2. Split products into high-efficiency, low-efficiency, and observation groups.
3. Mention keyword data as search context or automatic matching signal.
4. Recommend product bid strength up/keep/down.
5. Check price, reviews, benefits, thumbnail, detail page, stock, and margin for low-conversion products.

Client wording:

> G마켓 광고는 개별 키워드를 세밀하게 제외하거나 순위를 고정 관리하는 방식보다는, 상품을 기준으로 노출이 확장되고 개인화되는 구조에 가깝습니다. 따라서 차주 운영은 키워드별 세부 조정보다 상품별 광고비 소진, 구매 발생 여부, ROAS를 기준으로 입찰 강도를 조정하는 방향이 적합합니다.

## Nine Block-Style Franchise CPL PPT

Use for franchise recruitment, CPL lead-generation, and media-mix proposal decks that need a disciplined 14-slide B2B agency structure.

Core rules:

- Use 16:9 wide slides, 33.87cm x 19.05cm.
- Use exactly 14 slides when following the Nine Block reference.
- Keep margins at least 1.5cm.
- Use Pretendard Bold for titles and Pretendard Regular for body when available.
- Keep titles and body left-aligned.
- Avoid title underline decoration, top/bottom color bars, text-only slides, cream/beige backgrounds, meaningless shapes, and text overflow.

Story order:

1. Cover
2. Proposal overview
3. Budget summary comparison
4. Media budget detail
5. Budget rationale
6. Naver search strategy
7. Powerlink keyword strategy
8. Creative examples
9. Meta strategy
10. Google strategy
11. Kakao strategy
12. Meta vs Google DB use
13. Expected effects
14. Closing

For the full slide-by-slide production spec, load `nineblock-media-mix-proposal.md`.

## GabiaCNS Ad Marketer Judgment

- Separate possible advertising from meaningful advertising.
- Prefer one strong channel over a scattered media mix when the budget is small.
- For budgets under KRW 3,000,000/month, usually recommend one primary channel unless the funnel logic and data volume justify splitting.
- Diagnose before introducing GabiaCNS capabilities.
- Tie every media recommendation to objective, budget, landing readiness, conversion path, tracking, creative capacity, and lead follow-up.
- If the plan lacks landing, tracking, creative, or follow-up capacity, call it out before proposing a media mix.
- Do not present A/B testing as only ad-copy testing for complex performance problems; include keyword intent, bid/rank, landing, conversion path, and lead quality.

## Blog Image Production

Use for blog insert images, card-news images, and HTML/CSS image mockups.

- The image should summarize the article and refresh the reader's attention, not merely decorate the post.
- Prioritize information hierarchy: title, short message, comparison/checklist/scenario, conclusion.
- Prefer Pretendard.
- Avoid oversized text that escapes cards.
- Avoid artificial-looking icons, excessive decoration, unstable alignment, or low-information simple layouts.
- Remove meaningless progress bars or gauge bars unless they encode real data.

# Nine Block CPL Media-Mix Proposal Reference

Use this reference as a stable, low-ambiguity production guide for creating a 14-slide PowerPoint proposal for Nine Block, a cafe franchise brand. The proposal is for franchise recruitment CPL advertising media mix planning, delivered by Gabia CNS to an advertiser in a professional B2B agency proposal format.

## Technical Specification

- Slide size: 33.87cm x 19.05cm, 16:9 wide.
- Total slides: exactly 14.
- Margin: keep at least 1.5cm on all sides.
- Title font: Pretendard Bold; fallback Calibri Bold.
- Body font: Pretendard Regular; fallback Calibri.
- Title size: 28pt on content slides. Cover title may use 38pt as specified.
- Body size: maximum 14pt.
- Caption/label size: 11pt.
- Alignment: left-align titles and body. Do not use centered alignment.

## Color Palette

Use these exact colors:

- Main dark navy: `#1E2761`
- Light background: `#FFFFFF`
- Accent blue: `#4A90D9`
- Light blue-gray card background: `#E8EEF7`
- Primary text: `#1A1A2E`
- Secondary text: `#6B7280`
- Divider: `#D1D5DB`

Dark slides:

- Background: `#1E2761`
- Text: `#FFFFFF`
- Subtext/slide number accent: `#CADCFC`

Light slides:

- Background: `#FFFFFF`
- Title text: `#1E2761`
- Body text: `#1A1A2E`

## Absolute Prohibitions

- Do not use decorative underline bars below titles.
- Do not use top or bottom horizontal color bars.
- Do not create text-only slides.
- Do not use cream or beige backgrounds.
- Do not repeat the same layout for two consecutive slides.
- Do not use meaningless decorative circles, triangles, or arbitrary shapes.
- Do not allow text to overflow outside text boxes.
- Do not use centered alignment.

## Layout Vocabulary

- `2-column`: split slide left/right 50:50 with independent content in each area.
- `3-card`: three equal-width cards arranged horizontally, card gap 0.5cm.
- `table`: header row uses `#1E2761` background and white text; data rows alternate white and light gray.
- `icon-row`: left number or circular icon, diameter 1.2cm, `#4A90D9` fill, white text; right title plus description.
- `metric-callout`: centered 48pt bold number, 12pt label below, inside `#E8EEF7` card.
- `left-image-right-text`: left 40% color block or image, right 60% text.

## Common Repeating Elements

- Every slide: bottom-right slide number `01` through `14`, 11pt. Use `#CADCFC` on dark slides and `#6B7280` on light slides.
- Every light slide: top-left slide title, 22pt bold, `#1E2761`.
- Every light slide: one-line subtitle under title, 13pt, `#6B7280`.
- Separate title and subtitle by whitespace only. Do not add a line or underline.

## Slide-By-Slide Specification

### Slide 1: Cover, Dark Background

Background: full `#1E2761`.

Left 70%:

- Main title: `나인블럭 온라인광고 미디어믹스 제안서`, 38pt bold, `#FFFFFF`, maximum 2 lines.
- Subtitle: `가맹점 모집 CPL 광고 전략`, 18pt, `#CADCFC`, 0.8cm below title.
- Company: `Gabia CNS`, 14pt, `#FFFFFF`, 0.4cm below subtitle.

Right 30%:

- Stack four media tags vertically: `NAVER`, `META`, `GOOGLE`, `KAKAO`.
- Each tag: white text, 14pt, rectangle with 10% transparent white background.

Slide number: `01`, bottom right, 12pt, `#CADCFC`.

### Slide 2: Proposal Overview, Light Background

Layout: top summary plus three icon rows below.

Top summary:

- Text: `가맹 문의 가능성이 높은 검색 수요를 확보하고, 콘텐츠와 디스플레이 광고로 잠재 창업자를 확장합니다.`
- 15pt, `#1E2761`, inside `#E8EEF7` box with 0.5cm horizontal padding.

Bottom icon rows, split evenly into three vertical sections:

1. `01` / `검색 고의도 확보` / `네이버·카카오 검색광고로 가맹 문의 의도가 있는 사용자를 직접 유입`
2. `02` / `브랜드 신뢰 보강` / `브랜드검색과 파워콘텐츠로 공식 정보·블로그 콘텐츠 연결`
3. `03` / `잠재 창업자 확장` / `메타·구글·카카오 지면에서 발견형 리드와 리타겟팅 확대`

### Slide 3: Budget Summary Comparison, Light Background

Layout: `3-card`.

Card common spec:

- Top badge.
- Budget number, 40pt bold, `#1E2761`.
- Divider.
- Included media list.
- Operating note.
- Border: 1pt `#D1D5DB`.
- Corner radius: 0.3cm.
- Background: white.

Cards:

- Minimum: badge `테스트`, `#6B7280` background; budget `600만원`; note `데이터 제한`.
- Recommended: badge `추천`, `#4A90D9` background, white text; budget `1,000만원`; note `추천 운영안`; border 2pt `#4A90D9`.
- Maximum: badge `확장`, `#1E2761` background; budget `1,500만원`; note `적극 추천`.

### Slide 4: Media Budget Detail, Light Background

Layout: `table`.

Columns:

- `광고 상품`
- `최소안`
- `적정안`
- `최대안`
- `운영 역할`

Style:

- Header row: `#1E2761` background, `#FFFFFF` text, 12pt bold.
- Data rows: alternate white and `#F8FAFC`, 13pt.
- Total row: `#E8EEF7` background, bold text.
- Column width ratio: 25% / 10% / 10% / 10% / 45%.
- Footnote below table: `* VAT 포함 기준 / 구글·메타 운영 대행 수수료 별도`, 11pt, `#6B7280`.

### Slide 5: Budget Rationale, Light Background

Layout: `3-card`, implemented differently from Slide 3.

Each card:

- Top label: `최소안`, `적정안`, or `최대안`.
- Bold title.
- Body description, 2-3 lines.
- Same width and fixed same height.

Card styles:

- Minimum: `#F8FAFC` background, `#6B7280` label.
- Recommended: `#E8EEF7` background, `#4A90D9` label.
- Maximum: `#1E2761` background, all text `#FFFFFF`.

### Slide 6: Naver Search Advertising Strategy, Light Background

Layout: vertical `3-card`, three tall cards arranged left/center/right.

Cards:

- `파워링크`: top label `#1E2761` background, white text; operating purpose; three key content lines.
- `브랜드검색`: top label `#4A90D9` background, white text; operating purpose; three key content lines.
- `파워콘텐츠`: top label `#6B7280` background, white text; operating purpose; three key content lines.

### Slide 7: Powerlink Keyword Strategy, Light Background

Layout: `2-column`.

Left 45%: budget ratio visualization.

- Three rows: `최소안`, `적정안`, `최대안`.
- Each row: label plus horizontal stacked ratio bar.
- Segment colors: broad `#1E2761`, mid `#4A90D9`, narrow `#CADCFC`.

Right 55%: keyword example table.

- Columns: `범위`, `의도`, `키워드 예시`, `랜딩`.
- Use only 5-6 rows for readability.

### Slide 8: Creative Operation Examples, Light Background

Layout: 2 x 3 card grid.

- Use five creative cards and either leave the sixth cell as whitespace or use it for a short note.
- Each card: top-left type label badge, `#1E2761` small box; body ad copy.
- Card background: `#F8FAFC`.
- Border: 1pt `#D1D5DB`.
- Padding: 0.4cm.

### Slide 9: Meta Advertising Strategy, Light Background

Layout: `2-column`.

Left: campaign structure.

- Four `icon-row` items.
- Each item: campaign name plus one-line target/operation direction.

Right: lead-form A/B test.

- Subtitle: `리드폼 A/B 테스트`.
- Two small comparison cards:
  - `기본형 폼`: `문의 장벽 최소화`.
  - `고의도형 폼`: `창업 지역·예산 질문 포함`.
- Place `VS` text between the cards.

### Slide 10: Google Advertising Strategy, Light Background

Layout: top `2-column` plus full-width bottom section.

Top:

- Left: `PMax 구조`, with two asset groups as `icon-row` items.
- Right: `Demand Gen 구조`, with two asset groups as `icon-row` items.

Bottom:

- Subtitle: `수동 타겟 세그먼트`.
- Tags: `인마켓`, `관심분야`, `상세인구통계`, `맞춤세그먼트(검색어)`, `맞춤세그먼트(URL)`, `리마케팅`.
- Tag style: `#E8EEF7` background, `#1E2761` text, corner radius 0.2cm, 12pt.

### Slide 11: Kakao Advertising Operation, Light Background

Layout: `3-card`.

Cards:

- `검색광고`: inclusion badge `적정안·최대안`; operation content; role.

Layout: top statement plus seven icon rows.

Top:

### Slide 14: Closing CTA, Dark Background

Background: full `#1E2761`.

Left 60%:

- Main sentence: `나인블럭과 가비아CNS, 데이터 기반 가맹 모집 광고를 시작합니다`, 26pt bold, `#FFFFFF`, maximum 2 lines.
- Subtitle: `검색 의도와 발견형 리드를 연결하는 풀 채널 미디어믹스`, 14pt, `#CADCFC`.

Right 40%:

- Label: `담당자 연락처`, 12pt, `#CADCFC`.
- Contact area: white box, `#1E2761` text.
- Use placeholders such as `[담당자명]`, `[연락처]`, `[이메일]` when actual contact information is not provided.

## Final QA Checklist

Before delivering a deck built from this reference:

- Exactly 14 slides.
- Correct slide size, 16:9 wide.
- At least 1.5cm margin on all sides.
- No centered text.
- No title underline bars.
- No header/footer color bars.
- No cream/beige background.
- No text-only slide.
- No meaningless decoration shapes.
- No two consecutive slides reuse the same layout.
- Every slide has slide number `01` through `14`.
- Light slides have top-left title and subtitle.
- Table rows and budget totals match the source workbook.
- Text does not overflow any text box.

# B2B Custom Promotional Goods Search Ads

Use this reference for custom pouches, brand merchandise, beauty accessories, corporate promotional goods, OEM/ODM production, and other quote-based B2B manufacturing advertisers.

## Diagnosis

- Distinguish a quote-based custom manufacturer from a retail promotional-goods store.
- Visible product breadth is proof of production capability, but it can also make the site feel like a portfolio archive rather than a conversion landing page.
- A large portfolio does not replace decision information such as MOQ, production lead time, sample availability, printing method, packaging, design requirements, and quote inputs.

## Small-Budget Channel Principle

- Under KRW 1,000,000 per month, concentrate on one high-intent search channel.
- For Korean B2B custom production, Naver Powerlink is usually the first test when clear Korean search demand exists.
- Do not split the initial budget across Naver, Google, display, and content ads merely for media coverage.
- Power Content and Google Search are second-stage tests after high-intent terms and landing paths are validated.
- Shopping Search Ads are not the default when price and options are not standardized and the conversion requires specification discussion and a quote.

## Campaign Structure

Prefer service and search-intent groups:

1. Core specialized production, such as beauty-brand pouches.
2. Material or production-method terms, such as PVC, mesh, padding, OEM, or ODM.
3. Brand goods and MD production.
4. Corporate promotional goods and event merchandise.
5. Selected product-specific production.
6. Brand terms.

Do not primarily split groups by buyer type when the same broad term is used by several buyer types. Use ad copy and landing content to reflect the likely use case.

## Keyword Priority

- First: terms that combine product, audience/use, and production intent.
- Second: material, method, OEM/ODM, or brand-goods terms.
- Third: broad corporate promotional-goods terms with low bids and strict search-term review.
- Avoid treating generic `promotional goods`, `cheap`, `small quantity`, or individual-purchase traffic as equivalent to a qualified B2B inquiry.

## Landing Requirements

A search-ad landing page should answer:

- what can be made;
- minimum order quantity or consultation policy;
- typical production lead time;
- available materials, printing, logo, packaging, and design options;
- sample availability;
- production process;
- quote inputs;
- contact options.

Use product/category pages for specific search intent. Create two or three dedicated landing pages before attempting a full site redesign.

## KPI Framework

Measure:

1. click and CPC;
2. inquiry;
3. qualified inquiry with product, quantity, and delivery timing;
4. quote sent;
5. sample or specification discussion;
6. contract and revenue.

Do not optimize only to CTR or raw inquiry count. Small-budget B2B accounts should use qualified-inquiry cost and quote rate as intermediate KPIs.

## Operating Sequence

1. Audit current keywords, search terms, copy, landing pages, and conversion tracking.
2. Concentrate budget on high-fit product and production-intent groups.
3. Add negative keywords and reduce broad terms that attract retail or low-volume requests.
4. Link inquiry outcomes back to keyword and group.
5. Scale only groups producing qualified inquiries or quote requests.
6. Add content or a second search platform only after the primary structure is validated.

## Client-Facing Explanation

> 맞춤 제작 B2B 광고는 방문자를 많이 모으는 운영보다 실제 제작 수량과 납기, 사양을 갖춘 담당자의 문의를 선별하는 운영이 중요합니다. 따라서 초기에는 검색 의도가 구체적인 제품·소재·제작 키워드에 예산을 집중하고, 문의 이후 견적 가능 여부까지 확인해 예산을 재배분합니다.

## Confirmation Items

- Current media spend and recent search-term report
- Product priorities and unwanted orders
- MOQ and typical contract size
- Production lead time and rush-order policy
- Sample and design support
- Conversion-tracking readiness
- Inquiry, quote, contract, and revenue feedback process

## Core shift

Google Search is moving from a traditional query-results page toward AI-assisted, conversational, multimodal, and agentic experiences.

For marketers, the core implication is:

> Search marketing is no longer only about matching keywords to ads. It is increasingly about being eligible, useful, and measurable across AI Mode, AI Overviews, conversational shopping, product feeds, landing-page selection, YouTube discovery, and commerce checkout surfaces.

## Search and AI Mode

Official I/O 2026 points:

- AI Mode is positioned as Google's strongest AI Search experience.
- Search input is becoming multimodal: text, images, files, videos, and Chrome tabs.
- AI Overviews and AI Mode are being connected into a more seamless AI Search experience.
- Search agents can monitor web, blog, news, social, shopping, sports, and finance data for ongoing tasks.
- Search can generate custom layouts, tables, graphs, simulations, and mini-app-like experiences.
- Universal Cart adds a shopping layer across Search, Gemini, YouTube, Gmail, and more.

Marketing implication:

- SEO/SEM should prepare for conversational and multi-step search journeys, not only short keyword queries.
- Content should answer user intent clearly enough to be useful inside AI-generated search experiences.
- Product and feed data must be rich enough for AI systems to understand product context, attributes, suitability, offers, and checkout options.

## Search ads in the AI era

Google announced AI Search ad formats built with Gemini:

- Conversational Discovery ads: ads answer a person's specific question with tailored product/service context.
- Highlighted Answers: highly relevant ads can appear as highlighted answers in AI Mode recommendation-style lists.
- AI-powered Shopping ads: Gemini can pull relevant products and create explainers for complex product decisions.
- Business Agent for Leads: a smart brand agent inside the ad can answer questions from the advertiser's website and turn the interaction into a lead.
- Direct Offers expansion: promotional bundling, native checkout for eligible Universal Commerce Protocol merchants, and travel deal expansion.

Proposal implication:

- Search proposals should move from "keyword coverage" to "intent coverage + landing/content readiness + AI campaign controls."
- For lead generation, the Business Agent direction reinforces the need for accurate website content, FAQ content, conversion definitions, and lead-quality feedback.
- For ecommerce, product feeds, product descriptions, offers, checkout readiness, and Merchant Center quality become strategic inputs, not back-office setup.

Availability caution:

- Do not claim these formats are fully available to all advertisers in Korea unless availability is verified.
- Position them as Google's strategic direction and test-readiness items.

## AI Max

Important official points:

- AI Max is expanding beyond Search into Shopping campaigns and travel-specific formats.
- AI Max for Shopping uses Merchant Center feeds to transform product data into dynamic Shopping ads that can answer conversational queries.
- AI Brief lets advertisers guide AI with business context, messaging guidelines, matching guidelines, and audience guidelines.
- Final URL Expansion uses Google AI to match each query to the most relevant landing page.
- Text disclaimers are being introduced to keep mandatory text visible even when using Final URL Expansion.

Marketing implication:

- AI Max is not a "turn it on and win" feature. It requires business guidance, brand guardrails, exclusions, landing-page control, product/feed quality, and conversion-quality feedback.
- For regulated or claim-sensitive industries, AI Max should be paired with mandatory wording, claim restrictions, landing-page review, and preview checks.
- For exact/phrase-heavy Search accounts, AI Max can be framed as a controlled test for long-tail and conversational query expansion, but with experiments and brand controls.

## Shopping and agentic commerce

Important official points:

- Universal Cart can work across retailers and Google services such as Search, Gemini, YouTube, Gmail, and more.
- Universal Commerce Protocol can support checkout on Google via Google Pay or handoff to the merchant site, while the retailer remains merchant of record.
- UCP-powered checkout is expanding into ad campaigns such as Direct Offers and Shopping ads on YouTube with product feeds, for eligible integrations and markets.
- Merchant Center will gain AI performance insights to show how a brand performs on AI surfaces versus similar brands, initially in selected countries.
- Conversational attributes help retailers update product descriptions for conversational search behavior.

Marketing implication:

- Commerce proposals should include Merchant Center/feed diagnostics, product title and description quality, image/video asset quality, promotion data, product landing pages, and checkout readiness.
- Shopping ads and commerce SEO/AEO are converging around feed data, product attributes, AI discoverability, and checkout experience.

## Commerce-to-ads linkage details

When the user asks whether commerce and ads are being linked more tightly, use this interpretation:

- Merchant Center product data is becoming an active campaign input, not only a Shopping listing feed.
- AI Max for Shopping uses Merchant Center feeds to understand product context and generate dynamic Shopping ad responses for conversational queries.
- Final URL Expansion can connect a shopper's query or intent to the most relevant product or landing page.
- Ask Advisor can pull product details from Merchant Center to help set up campaigns in Google Ads, and Merchant Center is getting agentic assistance connected with Google Ads and Google Analytics.
- Demand Gen can use Merchant Center product videos and product feeds to distribute dynamic product videos based on real-time user interest.
- Direct Offers and UCP-powered checkout connect eligible products, promotions, product feeds, and checkout options more directly with ads and Google surfaces.
- Conversational attributes help update product descriptions for the way users search in AI Mode and Gemini, making feed/content quality part of ad discoverability.

Client-facing angle:

> Google commerce data is moving from "registered product information" to "AI-readable advertising material." Product titles, descriptions, images, videos, promotions, inventory, landing pages, and checkout options all become inputs that Google AI can use to match user intent, create ad context, select formats, and route users to the right purchase path.

Critical caution:

- This does not mean every retailer automatically gets native checkout, UCP, Direct Offers, or AI Mode Shopping exposure.
- Check country, account eligibility, Merchant Center status, product category, feed quality, policy approval, and Google Ads beta availability before making client-facing promises.
- For proposals, present these as readiness and testing directions unless the advertiser's Google account confirms access.

## YouTube and Demand Gen

Important official points:

- Demand Gen is positioned around YouTube, AI-powered relevance, creator partnership videos, and discovery before users actively search.
- Multimodal video creation in Asset Studio can help build YouTube assets.
- Creator partnership videos can be boosted during Demand Gen setup.
- Retailers can upload videos to Merchant Center and dynamically distribute product videos across Demand Gen campaigns based on real-time user interest.
- New Google Maps inventory, expanded checkout links, broader product-feed support, Campaign Type Attribution, Uplift Experiments, and privacy-safe third-party integrations were announced.

Marketing implication:

- Demand Gen should not be treated as only display/video reach.
- For ecommerce/product-heavy advertisers, Demand Gen needs product feeds, video assets, creator content, and attribution planning.
- For local businesses, Google Maps inventory may become relevant, but market/product availability must be verified.

## Creative automation: Asset Studio

Important official points:

- Asset Studio is described as a one-stop shop for developing creative assets in Google Ads.
- It can use a marketing brief, brand guidelines, website, and goals to generate assets across creative themes and asset types.
- It supports natural-language refinement.
- Gemini Omni integration is coming for video assets.
- 1-Click A/B Testing can test assets based on goals.
- Global English rollout is expected in summer 2026.

Marketing implication:

- Creative production becomes an ongoing optimization process rather than a one-time asset upload.
- Before using AI creative tools, prepare brand guidelines, required claims, prohibited wording, tone, product benefits, localization rules, and legal disclaimers.
- Human review remains essential for policy, tone, claims, localization, and legal-risk control.

## Ask Advisor and AI operations

Important official points:

- Ask Advisor connects AI agents across Google Ads, Analytics, Merchant Center, and Google Marketing Platform.
- It is intended to provide proactive recommendations, campaign setup assistance, insights, and next-step recommendations.
- It is currently beta for English-language accounts, with more features rolling out.

Marketing implication:

- Proposal language should not say "AI replaces the operator." The stronger position is "AI accelerates diagnosis and execution, while the agency designs goals, guardrails, tests, and business interpretation."

## Bidding and budgeting

Important official points:

- Journey-aware bidding is in beta for Search campaigns optimizing to Target CPA.
- It can learn from both biddable and non-biddable conversion goals across the lead-to-sales journey, such as phone calls, form submissions, newsletter signups, and more.
- Smart Bidding Exploration helps capture less obvious queries using ROAS tolerance.
- Smart Bidding Exploration is expanding to Performance Max and Shopping campaigns with product feeds.
- Campaign total budgets reduce manual budget adjustments versus daily budgets.
- Demand-led pacing will adjust spend to follow consumer demand on peak and slow days while staying within monthly budget and daily spending limits.

Marketing implication:

- Lead-generation proposals should define the full journey: raw lead, qualified lead, phone connection, visit/show, purchase/contract, and revenue.
- Smart bidding improves only when the advertiser provides meaningful conversion values and downstream quality signals.
- Budget recommendations should consider demand volatility and total-period budgets, not only fixed daily budgets.

## Measurement and data

Important official points:

- Google emphasizes data strength, Data Manager, Google tag gateway, no-code tag upgrades, Meridian GeoX, Meridian Studio, and GA360 as a growth command center.
- Google cited an average 14% conversion lift for Google tag gateway users in internal data.
- Data Manager will show data flows from sources such as BigQuery, Google Drive, HubSpot, and Shopify.
- Meridian GeoX supports geographic incrementality testing.
- Meridian is being brought into GA360 for unified first-party, cross-channel, and metric signals.
- Qualified Future Conversions, powered by Gemini, link upper-funnel spend to future sales via signals such as brand searches.

Marketing implication:

- Google Ads proposals should not rely only on last-click or platform-reported conversions.
- For larger advertisers, include tag health, first-party data, CRM/offline conversion import, incrementality, MMM, geo experiments, and full-funnel measurement.
- For smaller advertisers, scale the principle down: clean conversion tags, GA4, Google Ads conversion goals, call/form tracking, and CRM status feedback.

## Critical stance for proposals

Do:

- Frame AI as a tool that needs feed quality, first-party data, clear goals, conversion quality, and human guardrails.
- Explain that Google is moving toward AI-assisted Search, agentic commerce, AI-led campaign operations, and unified measurement.
- For ecommerce, prioritize Merchant Center feed quality, product descriptions, images/videos, promotions, and landing pages.
- For lead generation, prioritize full-funnel conversion definitions and offline/CRM feedback.
- For creative, build brand guidelines and claim restrictions before using AI asset generation.

Avoid:

- "AI Max will automatically solve performance."
- "AI Search ads are fully available everywhere."
- "Manual keywords no longer matter." Better: keyword work shifts toward query intent, exclusions, AI guardrails, and landing diagnostics.
- "Demand Gen is just YouTube reach."
- "UCP/Universal Cart means all merchants can check out inside Google now."
- "MMM or Meridian replaces campaign reporting." It complements, rather than replaces, tactical reports.
- Applying every new Google product to a low-budget advertiser at once. Choose the minimum viable test based on objective, budget, data readiness, and creative/feed readiness.

## How to check Korea availability and rollout timing

When the user asks when a Google I/O or Google Marketing Live feature will be available in Korea, do not infer from the keynote alone.

Use this check order:

1. Official announcement wording: look for "beta," "rolling out," "available in the U.S.," "global English," "eligible advertisers," "in coming months," or exact dates.
2. Google Ads Help / Merchant Center Help: check if the feature has a setup page and whether it names eligibility, prerequisites, country, language, campaign type, or linked-account requirements.
3. Google Ads or Merchant Center account UI: the most practical check is whether the toggle, tab, banner, upgrade tool, or recommendation appears in the actual Korean account.
4. Google Ads API release notes: if an API field/service exists, the feature is technically being productized, but this still does not guarantee UI availability for every advertiser or market.
5. Google Partner / Google Korea rep: for beta access, allowlist, or Korea rollout timing, this is often the only reliable source.

Client-facing wording:

> Google has announced the strategic direction and some rollout timelines, but Korea availability can differ by account, language, product, and beta eligibility. We recommend checking the advertiser's actual Google Ads and Merchant Center accounts first, then confirming unclear items through Google Partner or Google Korea channels before including them as executable features.

Examples:

- AI Max for Search has Help Center and API support, and Google announced DSA/ACA/campaign-level broad match auto-upgrade timing for September 2026 for eligible campaigns. This is more concrete than many GML announcements.
- Ads in AI Overviews can be eligible from existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns when conditions are met, but the exact serving and placement visibility may differ by market and query.
- Demand Gen product feeds already have Help Center setup instructions, but actual use still depends on Merchant Center prerequisites and account UI availability.
- UCP, Universal Cart, Direct Offers, AI Mode Shopping, Business Agent, Asset Studio, Ask Advisor, and some measurement products may be beta, market-limited, English-limited, eligible-account-limited, or rollout-stage features. Treat them as readiness items unless the account confirms access.

## 2026-07-30 update: target bidding, Display migration, and YouTube formats

### 1) 새로 학습한 사실

#### 공식 확인

- Starting 2026-08-17, Google is changing target-based bidding behavior for campaigns that are `Limited by budget`. The main affected strategies are Target CPA and Target ROAS, plus Target CPC for Demand Gen.
- Officially listed affected campaign types include Search, Shopping, Performance Max, Demand Gen, and Travel. The change also applies through Search Ads 360, and to Demand Gen through Display & Video 360.
- Google will not automatically change the advertiser's target or budget. A campaign that has historically overperformed its stated target may move closer to that target after the change. For example, an actual CPA materially below the entered target CPA may rise toward the entered target if no target adjustment is made.
- Google recommends reviewing affected campaigns by 2026-08-17. The Bid Target Adjustment Tool began rolling out from 2026-07-06.
  - Official sources checked 2026-07-30:
    - https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/17061251
    - https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/17125145
- Google began a phased rollout of the tool for migrating eligible Display campaigns to Demand Gen in June 2026. The official Help page says new Display campaign creation and remaining automatic migration will occur `later`; it does not confirm the uploaded report's `late 2026` and `late 2027` dates.
- The recommended migration tool can transfer up to 42 days of source-campaign learning and reduce the expected new learning period to about 1-2 days. Migrated ads require review again, and unsupported settings/assets may need correction.
  - Official source checked 2026-07-30: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/17051545
- Target Frequency campaigns officially support mixing bumper, skippable in-stream, and non-skippable in-stream formats. Non-skippable cannot be combined with in-feed or Shorts in the same mixed setup.
  - Official source checked 2026-07-30: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/12400225

#### 제공 파일 기반 사실 - 계정/구매 가능 여부 재확인 필요

- The July 2026 Google/YouTube update file describes a mobile Shoppable Masthead with 4-6 related product cards, requiring Merchant Center product-feed connection.
- It also describes real-time Shorts previews for VVC/VRC, comments on Creator Partnership boost ads, and a strategic High SOV plus category-exclusion model replacing the former 100% SOV channel takeover structure.
- These are useful product directions, but exact Korea availability, reservation eligibility, account UI, lead time, and sales conditions require Google/rep confirmation.

### 2) 기존 지식에서 수정할 점

- Do not summarize the target-bidding update as `budget increases will no longer reduce performance`. It applies specifically to budget-constrained campaigns using affected target strategies, and campaigns that were outperforming a loose target may see CPA/ROAS move toward the entered target.
- Do not include Display and Hotel as newly affected campaign types based only on the uploaded report. Use the current official eligibility list unless Google updates it.
- Do not state that new Display creation stops in late 2026 or that automatic migration occurs in late 2027 as confirmed policy. The official Help wording is currently `coming later`.
- Do not create a duplicate Demand Gen campaign while keeping an identical Display campaign running without a transition plan. Google says the historical Display campaign can retain a learning advantage and suppress the new duplicate's ramp-up.
- Do not assume that every Display setting migrates. Lead forms, click-to-call assets, some exclusions, shared budgets, product filters, disclaimers, and other settings can block or alter migration.

### 3) 실무 적용 원칙

1. Before 2026-08-17, filter for `Limited by budget` campaigns using Target CPA, Target ROAS, or Demand Gen Target CPC.
2. Compare entered target versus recent actual performance. If the campaign is materially outperforming the target, decide whether to:
   - align the target to recent actual performance,
   - set a custom profitable target,
   - keep the target and accept movement toward it,
   - or use Maximize conversions/value when the budget is strictly fixed.
3. Allow at least 1-2 conversion cycles before judging the post-change result, especially for long conversion-delay businesses.
4. Inventory all Display campaigns and classify them as migrate now, repair before migration, observe, or retire.
5. Prefer the official migration tool where eligible so recent learning can transfer. Recheck channel controls, logos, goals, audiences, bidding, attribution windows, and approval status after migration.
6. Treat Shoppable Masthead and creator/takeover products as reserved or eligible-account products, not as default recommendations for small budgets.

### 4) 제안서/리포트 문장 예시

> Google은 2026년 8월 17일부터 예산 제한 상태의 목표 CPA·목표 ROAS 캠페인이 입력한 목표에 더 가깝게 운영되도록 입찰 동작을 변경합니다. 현재 실제 성과가 설정 목표보다 좋은 캠페인은 목표값을 그대로 둘 경우 성과가 목표 수준으로 이동할 수 있으므로, 적용 전 최근 실적과 손익 기준을 비교해 목표값을 재점검하겠습니다.

> 기존 Google 디스플레이 캠페인은 디멘드젠 내 디스플레이 네트워크로 이전되는 방향이 공식화됐습니다. 다만 일괄 전환 시점을 단정하기보다 계정에 이전 도구가 제공되는지와 미지원 설정을 먼저 확인하고, 가능한 경우 최근 학습을 이전하는 공식 도구를 활용해 공백을 줄이겠습니다.

### 5) 다음 확인 필요사항

- Actual post-2026-08-17 behavior in each advertiser account and any transition volatility.
- Korea availability and booking requirements for Mobile Shoppable Masthead.
- Korea/account availability of Shorts live preview, Creator Partnership comments, and updated channel takeover products.
- Google's future confirmed dates for blocking new Display creation and automatic migration.
- Unsupported-feature list changes in the Display-to-Demand Gen migration tool.

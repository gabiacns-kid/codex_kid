# Naver Ad Product Explanation Patterns

Source learned from an external agency training PDF: `네이버의 광고상품 개론(배포용).pdf`, reviewed 2026-07-01.

Use this reference when explaining Naver ad products to non-expert advertisers or building proposal decks that need to connect customer journey, ad products, and operating strategy.

## What To Learn From The Material

The strongest part of the material is not new platform facts, but the explanation structure:

1. Start with the customer's journey, not the ad product menu.
2. Place each Naver ad product by customer state.
3. Explain media mix as role division, not as "add another ad."
4. Translate automated products into the few controllable levers advertisers still have.
5. Explain display/GFA through creative and customer state, not only placement.
6. Warn against evaluating every product in isolation when the media mix is meant to work as a portfolio.

## Funnel-Based Explanation

Use this sequence when introducing Naver ads:

- Awareness: customers do not know the brand or need yet.
- Exploration: customers search category terms and compare options.
- Decision: customers search brand, product, store, price, or local intent.
- Conversion / repurchase: customers return, purchase, visit, or buy again.

Then map products by role:

- Search ads / Shopping Search: capture explicit demand.
- Brand Search / defensive Powerlink: protect brand or decision-stage traffic.
- Power Content: support information-searching users when an allowed keyword and suitable content exist.
- Place ads / local products: useful when offline visit is a meaningful conversion path.
- ADVoost / automated product ads: expand discovery and find new opportunities around products or assets.
- GFA / Naver display: create intent, reconnect non-search users, or support retargeting.

Good client-facing wording:

> Naver ads should not be explained as separate products to buy one by one. Each product works at a different point in the customer's journey. Search ads capture existing demand, display ads create or recover demand, and automated product ads help expand the funnel around products that can convert.

## ADVoost / Automation Explanation

When explaining automated Naver products, avoid saying "automatic means no operation."

A useful frame:

- The platform may automate bids, keywords, placements, or delivery.
- The advertiser still controls structure, budget, assets, exclusions where available, and interpretation.

Explain controllable levers:

1. Budget allocation
   - How much to give the automated system.
   - How quickly to scale.
   - Which product group deserves learning budget.

2. Asset / product grouping
   - Separate high-margin and low-margin products.
   - Separate seasonal products and always-on products.
   - Separate new products that need initial exposure.
   - Exclude poor stock, weak pages, low margin, or unsuitable products where possible.

3. Time density
   - Read when sales or inquiry intent is strongest.
   - Concentrate budget by season, weekday, hour, or promotion period where the platform allows.

Good wording:

> Automated advertising does not remove strategy. It changes the strategy from direct keyword control to budget, asset grouping, and timing control. The operator's job is to give the AI the right products, enough but not excessive budget, and a clean structure to learn from.

## GFA / Display Explanation

Do not describe GFA only as "Naver's Meta." That is partially useful, but incomplete.

Better frame:

- Search ads meet users who already have intent.
- GFA meets users while they are consuming content.
- Therefore, GFA sits between pure branding and pure performance.
- It can create demand, recover visitors, or test messages before demand becomes search.

Creative-first principle:

- Search ads are led by keyword intent.
- GFA is led by creative and message.
- If the first image or line does not stop attention, the campaign can spend without meaningful memory or action.

Good wording:

> In search ads, the keyword is the main character. In GFA, the creative is the main character. Users are not searching for us at that moment, so the image and copy must create the reason to stop, notice, and click.

## Shopping Search / ADVoost / GFA Triangle

For commerce proposals, explain the three-product structure as complementary:

- Shopping Search: captures users with explicit shopping/search intent.
- ADVoost Shopping / automated product ads: discovers broader product opportunities and new customer entry points.
- GFA product recommendation / dynamic display: brings back users or supports personalized product recommendation where feed/tracking readiness exists.

Budget explanation should start from total budget, then ratio:

- Do not say each product needs separate additional budget without context.
- First define the total monthly budget ceiling.
- Then allocate ratio by objective and stage.

General starting point, if tracking/feed/readiness is sufficient:

- Search / Shopping Search as the stable conversion base.
- Automated product ads as exploration or expansion budget.
- GFA/dynamic display as recovery or demand-generation budget.

But do not force this triangle when:

- conversion tracking is not ready;
- product feed is weak;
- budget is too small;
- creative assets are not prepared;
- the advertiser needs only immediate high-intent capture.

## Proposal Writing Pattern

Use this structure in client-facing proposals:

1. Customer journey diagnosis
   - "Customers do not all enter at purchase-ready stage."

2. Current missing stage
   - "Search captures demand, but cannot create demand."
   - "Display can create demand, but it needs creative and tracking."

3. Product role map
   - Show which ad product handles which customer state.

4. Operating levers
   - Keywords and landing for search.
   - Product/asset grouping and budget for automated ads.
   - Creative and audience for display.

5. Budget logic
   - Total budget first.
   - Ratio by role second.
   - Test, stop, and scale criteria third.

6. Caution
   - Tracking, feed, creative, landing, and audience readiness decide whether the product is meaningful.

## Useful Phrases To Reuse

- "광고상품을 추가하는 것이 아니라, 고객 여정에서 비어 있는 단계를 채우는 것입니다."
- "검색광고는 이미 생긴 의도를 잡고, 디스플레이는 아직 검색하지 않은 수요를 만듭니다."
- "자동화 광고는 운영이 사라지는 것이 아니라, 운영 포인트가 키워드에서 예산·그룹·소재로 이동하는 것입니다."
- "성과는 단일 상품 ROAS만이 아니라, 전체 퍼널에서 어떤 역할을 했는지 함께 봐야 합니다."
- "예산은 상품별로 더하는 방식이 아니라, 전체 예산 안에서 역할별 비중을 나누는 방식으로 설계해야 합니다."

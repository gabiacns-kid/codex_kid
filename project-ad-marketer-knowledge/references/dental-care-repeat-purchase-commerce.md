# Dental Care / Repeat-Purchase Commerce Proposal Pattern

Use this reference when preparing proposals for oral-care, hygiene, low-unit-price repeat-purchase goods, smart-store sellers, Gmarket/Auction sellers, or consumer goods where product specs, bundles, reviews, and recurring purchase behavior drive performance.

## Core Proposal Lesson

The strong proposal does not begin with media products. It begins by defining the category logic:

> Oral-care products such as toothbrushes and dental floss are daily necessities, but they behave like high-consideration consumer goods because buyers compare function, specs, use case, trust signals, bundles, and price-per-unit before purchase.

From that category diagnosis, derive channel role, keyword strategy, product-feed strategy, creative messaging, review strategy, and seasonal operation.

## Category Diagnosis Framework

For toothbrush, floss, and dental-care goods, check these five dimensions first:

1. Repeat-purchase cycle
   - Toothbrushes are replaced periodically.
   - Floss and hygiene supplies can recur monthly depending on usage habit.
   - First purchase experience can create long-term brand retention.
   - Multi-pack and bulk purchase offers are easier to justify than in many discretionary categories.

2. Functional/spec comparison
   - Buyers compare bristle type, bristle softness, head size, grip, age/use case, orthodontic/implant/denture use, and gum sensitivity.
   - Product pages and ad copy should state the functional reason to choose the product, not only the price.

3. Gift and group-purchase extension
   - Teacher's Day, Parents' Day, school/kindergarten, corporate gifts, and institutional supply can create bundle and set demand.
   - Name-printed toothbrushes, cups, tumblers, and packaged sets can expand beyond ordinary retail demand.

4. Price sensitivity and value comparison
   - Buyers compare unit price, pack count, shipping, coupons, free delivery, and bundle benefits.
   - Show "price per unit" or pack economy where possible.

5. Health and trust trend
   - Gum health, whitening, implant care, orthodontic care, and dentist-recommended framing can increase intent.
   - Be careful with unverified medical or certification claims. Use only claims that are true and visible on landing/product pages.

## Naver Role Split

For Naver, split roles by funnel and intent:

| Product | Role | Best Use | Core KPI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Powerlink | Brand defense + functional/informational exploration | Dentist-recommended toothbrush, gum-sensitive toothbrush, orthodontic toothbrush recommendation, floss use method | CTR, CPC, assisted purchase, brand search lift |
| Shopping Search Ads | Direct purchase capture | Brand/product/pack-count keywords, high-intent commerce keywords, product-specific searches | CVR, ROAS, order volume, product-level efficiency |
| ADVoost / performance display | Expansion or retargeting after product/search baseline exists | Additional reach, shopping target pools, retargeting, product expansion | Incremental conversions, CPA/ROAS, audience growth |

Suggested starting split when Naver is the main commerce channel:

- Shopping Search Ads and ADVoost: 65-75%
- Powerlink: 25-35%

Do not use this ratio blindly. Reduce Powerlink if it cannot produce qualified traffic or if budget is too small. Increase Shopping Search when product feed, reviews, price competitiveness, and category fit are strong.

## Keyword Intent Tiers

Build the proposal around intent tiers, not a flat keyword list.

| Tier | Intent | Example | Operation |
|---|---|---|---|
| High intent | Purchase or brand purchase | brand toothbrush, fine-bristle toothbrush 12-pack, floss bulk buy | Shopping Search focus, higher bids, strongest product pages |
| Mid intent | Comparison and use-case exploration | orthodontic toothbrush recommendation, implant toothbrush, gum toothbrush comparison | Powerlink + Shopping Search, function-led copy |
| Low intent | Education and early exploration | toothbrush replacement cycle, how to use floss, gum health care | Small Powerlink or SEO/blog content, do not overbid |

Always include:

- Brand keywords: defend against leakage to competitors and retailers; generally lower CPC, high ROAS, but still monitor bid inflation.
- Use-case keywords: implant, orthodontic, molar, denture, gum-sensitive, kids, kindergarten, institutional supply.
- Exclusions: electric toothbrush, toothbrush sterilizer, toothbrush holder, and non-carried adjacent items where they waste spend.

## Powerlink Copy Pattern

For dental-care goods, copy should combine:

- Brand
- Product function/spec
- Use case or pain point
- Trust signal
- Shipping/benefit where true

Example title structure:

> [Brand] fine-bristle toothbrush 12-pack | ultra-soft gum care

Example description structure:

> For sensitive gums. Orthodontic, implant, and dental-care lineups available. Fast shipping and bundle benefits.

Rules:

- Match ad-message to landing-page headline. If the ad says "fine bristle" or "gum care," the landing top area must repeat that message.
- Use numbers, reviews, certifications, dentist recommendation, or shipping claims only when verifiable.
- For seasonal offers, create separate seasonal copy rather than mixing season messages into all evergreen groups.

## Shopping Search Product Feed Strategy

Shopping Search Ads are product-led. Product registration quality is part of ad strategy.

Product name structure:

> [Brand] + core keyword + pack count/spec + primary attribute/use case

Example:

> [Brand] fine-bristle toothbrush 12-pack ultra-soft gum-care adult toothbrush orthodontic

Avoid:

- Keyword stuffing that reads like spam.
- Repeated near-duplicate product registrations that can create shopping exposure or duplication risk.
- Mismatched category or attributes.
- Misleading representative images or price/option mismatch.

Tag strategy:

- Use up to 10 meaningful tags when the platform allows it.
- Review and refresh tags every 2-4 weeks based on search-term, trend, and product-performance changes.

Thumbnail strategy:

1. Main image: product pack/count visible at a glance.
2. Detail image: bristle/head/function close-up.
3. Trust/use image: use case, recommendation, certification, or review signal if allowed and true.

## Review and Repurchase Strategy

For repeat-purchase goods, media efficiency depends on product-page conversion and customer retention.

Proposal should include non-media operation:

- Review coupon automation after purchase confirmation.
- Additional benefit for photo reviews.
- Bundle configuration such as 6+6, 12+2, set packages, and free-shipping thresholds.
- Smart Store customer/regular-buyer coupon or repurchase reminder where available.
- Monitor review quality and count by hero product.

Do not make the proposal only a CPC-management document. Explain how reviews, bundles, and repurchase logic reduce the cost of repeat revenue.

## Gmarket Power Click and AI SalesUp Pattern

For Gmarket, frame strategy around platform behavior:

- Power Click: captures high-intent purchase searches and price-comparison users.
- AI SalesUp: expands to automatically matched, exploratory, and repurchase-potential audiences where product data quality is strong.

Suggested role split:

| Product | Role |
|---|---|
| Power Click | High-intent keyword/search capture, direct bid operation, price-sensitive users |
| AI SalesUp | New customer discovery, repeat-purchase audience expansion, automated placement/search expansion |

Possible starting budget split:

- Power Click: 50-60%
- AI SalesUp: 40-50%

Condition this on:

- Product feed quality
- Product image quality
- Price competitiveness
- Review count
- Seasonal promotion readiness
- Whether recent Gmarket policy/UI allows the described controls. Verify current official guide before finalizing.

## Seasonal Planning

Dental-care repeat-purchase goods are evergreen, but promotions can create peaks.

Example season logic:

| Season | Demand | Operation |
|---|---|---|
| Parents' Day / Teacher's Day | Gift sets, oral-care gifts | Pre-register seasonal keywords, set packaging, Power Click focus |
| Summer travel | Portable toothbrush, family toothbrush | Travel bundle and portable set creatives |
| Lunar New Year / Chuseok | Gift sets, bulk purchase | Marketplace event participation and set product exposure |
| Major platform events | Big Smile Day, Black Friday, shopping festival | Increase budget before peak so automated products can learn |
| Evergreen | 12-pack fine-bristle, kindergarten gifts | Maintain efficient hero products and repurchase targeting |

## Integrated Channel Positioning

Do not present Naver and Gmarket as duplicate stores. Define their different customer roles:

| Channel | Customer Role | Goal | Content/Offer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Naver Smart Store | Brand-aware, function-searching, review-checking buyers | Conversion, review accumulation, repurchase | Function detail, reviews, SEO/blog support |
| Gmarket/Auction | Price-comparison and marketplace-event buyers | New customer volume, event sales | Unit price, discount, bundle, event participation |

## Monthly Operation Checklist

Use this as a recurring management section in proposals:

- Keyword CPC and quality score or platform-equivalent performance.
- Product-level ROAS and CVR.
- Low-ROAS products: bid down, exclude, or fix product page.
- Hero-product review count and photo-review volume.
- Competitor price, bundle, shipping, and coupon monitoring.
- Search-term and irrelevant matched-query review.
- Product title, tag, thumbnail, pack-count, and benefit refresh.
- Season 6-8 weeks ahead: product bundle, event application, seasonal keywords, creative copy, budget pre-scaling.

## Critical Cautions

- The learned proposal includes strong logic, but some platform claims are time-sensitive. Verify current Gmarket AI SalesUp, Power Click, search expansion, and Naver Shopping/ADVoost conditions from official sources before presenting as current fact.
- Do not claim "AI targeting efficiency is high" as guaranteed. Say repeat-purchase and product-rich categories can be suitable for automated expansion if product feed, price, reviews, and conversion history are strong.
- If budget is limited, do not force Naver + Gmarket + AI product mix all at once. Prioritize the channel where product feed/reviews/conversion path are already strongest.
- Medical/dental-adjacent claims require caution. Avoid unverifiable "dentist recommended," "medical certification," or treatment-effect claims unless evidence exists and landing page supports it.

## Proposal Quality Bar Learned

A high-quality proposal should include:

1. Category nature diagnosis before media recommendation.
2. Purchase psychology and repeat-purchase logic.
3. Channel role split by funnel and customer type.
4. Keyword tiers by intent strength.
5. Product-feed and thumbnail improvement.
6. Review, bundle, and repurchase strategy.
7. Marketplace-specific operation logic.
8. Seasonal calendar and pre-scaling timing.
9. Monthly checklist and KPI.
10. Verification/caution notes for platform rules and claims.
